# Exact signed Green paths and complete prime-power blocks

The coefficient-one inequality `K<=I`, equivalently `P>=N`, remains
unproved. The results below use the unchanged theta source, the complete
Gamma form, and every actual prime power.

- [Signed-load cut-current factorization](SIGNED_LOAD_CUT_CURRENT_FACTORIZATION.md): an exact positive prefix-current factorization of the reference Green inverse, its whole-space completion, and a uniformly invertible bidiagonal realization. Applied to the full aggregate correction, it retains all signed mixed interactions.
- [Grouped prime powers](GROUPED_PRIME_POWER_FACTORIZATION.md): exact hierarchical forward variances, the reflected paired-fiber mean mismatch, and the signed geometric-covariance factorization. The remaining Gamma/pole payment is stated explicitly, not claimed.
- [Prime-two mass imbalance](PRIME_TWO_MASS_IMBALANCE.md): the rigorous actual-theta bound `H_2(3 log(2)/4)<-2/3`, including complete infinite-tail estimates. It invalidates only the naive first-variance payment, not the full sign.
- [Summable-prime-block obstruction](PRIME_BLOCK_POSITIVITY_OBSTRUCTION.md): no decomposition into nonnegative blocks can allocate fixed nonnegative Gamma/pole portions among summable arithmetic blocks, including individual primes with all their powers. The proof uses exact neutral jets and their ordinary odd-L2 completeness; it asserts no negativity of the complete form.

Run `python check_factorizations.py` with Python 3.12 or later. Its
standard-library exact rational assertions check the finite algebra and
the elementary bounds in the prime-two infinite-tail proof. Finite
algebra checks supplement, rather than replace, the displayed general
proofs. No external data or installed numerical package is needed.

[the full-prime reference](../20261013_full_prime_green_reference/REPORT.md),
[its inverse](../20261013_full_prime_green_reference/REFERENCE_INVERSE.md),
and [the signed correction](../20261013_full_prime_green_reference/CORRECTION_WITH_SIGNED_PATHS.md).
