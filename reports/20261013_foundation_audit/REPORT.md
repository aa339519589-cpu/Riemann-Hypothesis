# Foundation audit and the remaining critical sign

Date: 2026-10-13. This independent review checks the original operator inputs used by the spectral route. It supplies an explicit parity- and pole-restricted Weil criterion. It does not prove the critical inequality or RH.

The parts have distinct scopes:

- [Closed forms and domains](CLOSED_FORM.md): the maximal jump-form domain, the closed Gamma form, the complete bounded physical prime perturbation, the even and odd compact-smooth cores, the source identity and the normalized ground. The unweighted prime-shift sum is not declared bounded.
- [Negative spectrum](NEGATIVE_SPECTRUM.md): the exact infinite-dimensional compact-map argument giving finite rank at every fixed negative depth and compact negative part. This does not make the total negative rank finite.
- [Even pole-free Weil criterion](EVEN_WEIL_CRITERION.md): real even compact tests with both pole moments zero suffice for RH, and this test class is form-dense in the original even ground-orthogonal space. Finite interpolation and convolution powers pay the entire remaining zero sum with multiplicity.
- [Whole negative-band regularity](NEGATIVE_BAND_REGULARITY.md): full paired-prime summability, actual-domain commutators, a noncircular low-frequency bound, and the Lyapunov estimate for arbitrary mixtures in each positive-depth band.
- [Theta strip and eigenstate tails](THETA_STRIP_AND_TAILS.md): the holomorphic square root of the actual theta multiplier, full-prime spatial localization, sharp-cutoff form domains, and fixed-order weighted derivative and Weil passages. The exact rational strip checker is replayed; the finite-height interval ledger is not.

The resulting equivalence is
\[
\mathrm{RH}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal E(q)\ge\tfrac12\operatorname{Var}_\nu(q)
\quad\text{for every real even }q\text{ in the maximal form domain}.
\]
The right-hand inequality is still unproved. Domain equivalence, compactness, and a finite-depth certificate do not establish its critical endpoint.

This review does not replay the numerical interval ledgers, certify critical-depth regularity or every later Schatten-class estimate, restore missing historical source records, or constitute proof-assistant verification. Those claims must be tracked separately; the present audit is limited to the explicitly stated original-form and criterion chain.
