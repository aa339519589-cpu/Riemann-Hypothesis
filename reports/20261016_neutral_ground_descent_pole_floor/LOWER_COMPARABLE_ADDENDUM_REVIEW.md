## Checked proof

1. The complete logarithmic-bin estimate (A5) retains every Mangoldt
   label. Reflection symmetry and Tonelli prove (A6) for nonnegative
   measurable sources. Compact interval indicators give local
   integrability of Rh, including the wall, without a pointwise
   regularity assumption.

2. The global lower comparison h >= c psi gives q = e^2/h <= C psi,
   bounded r_h = e/h, and r_h tending to zero. Symmetry then proves
   automatic finite reflected cost from U(h) < infinity. In particular,
   no prime-number-theorem asymptotic for the variable h or Rh is used.

3. The compact-strip translated-theta envelopes satisfy the same complete
   bin estimate. They dominate both rows of the generating functional,
   giving holomorphy and the claimed finite-jet Taylor coefficients.
   The generating translates are not assigned an original energy domain
   or used as large-shift ground variations.

4. PNT is used only for the smooth translated theta seed. The half-average
   convention gives 1/8 in (A11) and U(q)/4 in (A12). The absolute bound
   in (A11), followed by full-row symmetry, controls the entire tail of
   the second row by eta^2 U(h). Its remaining compact part is paid by
   local integrability of Rh and the theta-small seed. Thus the positive
   asymptotic (A15) is justified for measurable h.

5. A sufficiently high finite even jet, plus a multiple of psi, gives
   the positive completion (A16). The inequality B >= A^2 proves
   h_t >= 3h/4 for all real t. The exact difference quotient (A17) has
   the displayed integrable majorant, by the same full-row bounds.
   Therefore the nonzero Taylor coefficient yields an actual negative
   derivative of the reflected cost, not merely a formal variation.

## Scope

The written theorem passes for the stated globally lower-comparable
weak-neutral grounds with finite U(h). It does not classify grounds
outside that comparison class, prove attainment or compactness, or
establish a new complete ground identity for arbitrary measurable h.
It gives no original signed-prime upper bound, transfer of the original
numerical spectral threshold, or RH conclusion.
