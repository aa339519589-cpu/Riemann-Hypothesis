# Independent mathematical review

## Verdict

The package correctly constructs a common-noise complete Gamma-plus-prime
operational flow, proves its quantitative full-prime tail distance contraction
and finite almost-sure pairwise coalescence, and extends its L2 current
*differences* to any fixed finite theta-jet neutral repair. Its exact original
signed form, covariance costs, physical edge measures and weak pair adjoint
are retained. These results do not prove the remaining original-form sign or
K<=1.

The reviewed scope is fixed source depth and fixed finite neutral degree.
Neither uniformity in the degree nor finite expected coalescence time is
claimed. Polynomial neutral corrections need not have separately convergent
infinite currents.

## Independent checks

1. **Operational origin and common flow.** Multiplying the original generator
   by the complete theta_c removes its vanishing origin denominator. The
   boundary has the stated finite exponential holding rate. Its nonzero
   holding current is retained even though the physical clock collapses it.
   The pre-exit energy estimate and a genuine state-free exit channel give
   a uniform finite current variance. The shared destination/mark field has
   the stated Osgood jump-map modulus; the summable full-prime Lipschitz terms,
   local cutoff convergence and measurable subsequential construction give
   one joint field, not incompatible pairwise couplings. A common null set
   over all uncountably many starts is not required.

2. **All-prime contraction.** The continuous distance drift is exactly
   -1/2 times the theta integral between the states. The prime bound includes
   lower-only jumps that reverse their order. The corrected derivative
   c'-c contains its +15 term, and the complete divisor regrouping is
   positive and absolutely convergent. The exact rational checker was
   independently executed and passed. Its all-integer bound
   5368709120000/14719995925619<3/8 gives the claimed margin 1/8 without a
   finite-prime surrogate.

3. **Actual coalescence.** The compact entrance is bounded by a genuine
   Poisson-arrow stopping time. After entrance the distance is a
   nonnegative supermartingale, so its paths have bounded gap almost surely.
   On any bounded-gap tail the common-arrow intensity dominates a marginal
   physical clock density. That clock has infinite total time. Stopping
   both the gap and the at-most-one coalescence counting process justifies
   the compensator argument and proves finite pairwise meeting. The proof
   does not condition at an unproved last-prime stopping time and does not
   confuse Wasserstein drift with coalescence.

4. **Polynomial neutral bridges.** For every fixed degree, differentiating
   the continuous divided difference gives the displayed weighted negative
   drift beyond a finite threshold. The entire asynchronous prime row is
   exponentially smaller there. The pair carre is controlled by
   A_(2m-1). The stronger D_(2m+1) expectation controls the terminal L2
   tail, so almost-sure meeting is not incorrectly used as uniform
   integrability. The stopped martingale consequently gives the exact mean
   and variance of the finite pre-meeting integral. For the j-th theta jet,
   the polynomial degree is 2j, bracket exponent 4j-1, and terminal-control
   exponent 4j+1. No moment of the absolute meeting state is presumed.

5. **Projection and full signed accounting.** The deterministic neutral
   component is removed by the common N projection, but its random bridge
   noise and all mixed covariance remain. The corrected source retains
   -alpha sinh(x/2)/2-sum beta_j delta W s_j. The bounded-current formula
   has precisely the coefficients +8C_P-2C_H-2(1-delta)C_N. The polynomial
   formula retains +V_H+(1-delta)V_N-V_P and never forms individually
   divergent polynomial J or C. Positive semidefiniteness of C signs C_P
   and C_H but not the reflected C_N or the complete combination.

6. **Physical measures and weak adjoint.** The ordered Gamma, prime-forward,
   pole, Gamma-wall and prime-reflected measures have the stated half-line
   factors. The upper-only and common absorption channels sum to the full
   displayed loss in the pair adjoint. Both Cauchy partial fractions and
   all cross-row signs check. Reflected-curve incoming densities have
   Jacobians 1/w and 1/z, and prime transitions include both label maps
   n->nk and n->nk^2, with order reversal. The singular adjoint expressions
   are only used with common cutoffs in weak pairing, not asserted as
   separate convergent rows or strong-domain identities.

## What remains unresolved

The coalescing flow supplies legal, finite, same-source bridge energies and
an actual all-prime tail coupling estimate. It does not compare the original
signed edge measures. The positive storage theorem and the edge-variance
terms have an exact compensating debit; they cannot be counted as two
independent favorable budgets. No pathwise membership of the random bridge
field in the original P completion, or a favorable sign of its complete
variance combination, is established by this package.
