# Mathematical and numerical review of the positive-factor theorem

[REPORT.md](REPORT.md) establishes a nonnegative bounded factor B, a nonnegative unbounded form A, and a legal bounded comparison Q for the exact original K=A-B. The full-prime positivity certificate was independently rerun at 60 decimal digits, with rational box bounds identical to the original 35-digit execution. A separate analytic review checks the convolution, all complete tails and the reducing-ground argument. The weighted comparison and original-domain form-sandwich were independently reviewed in all seven sections. The concluding bound is 0<=Q<=2^26 I, with eigenvalue 1 of infinite multiplicity; Q<=I remains unproved.

## Review I. Complete factor and interval certificate

## 1. Original factors and domains

The complete prime jump form is d_p-Vp and the first Gamma exponential jump form is (k1*Phi)/(2h)-m(k1*)m, where k1(x)=exp(-|x|/2). The latter has multiplier 4-1/(t^2+1/4), with no missing factor two. Their subtraction from .5(I-Pi_e0) gives exactly B=D_B+Vp+m(k1*)m-.5Pi_e0. The complementary complete Gamma jump kernel is nonnegative. Its form A has the original full Gamma form domain because the subtracted first-exponential form is bounded. B is bounded self-adjoint, so K=A-B has the corresponding original operator domain by bounded perturbation.

Constant original q=v/e0 has zero jump energies, and therefore B e0=0 and C e0=.5e0 for C=B+.5Pi_e0. Once C>=0 is established, this exact reducing eigenvector justifies subtracting .5Pi_e0. An arbitrary positive operator minus a projection would not suffice. On e0-perp the lower bound is (1-r) integral D_B|v|^2. The upper bound B<=.5(I-Pi_e0) follows from the two nonnegative subtracted jump forms. D_B>0 everywhere implies ker B=span(e0), without implying a uniform unweighted positive gap.

An operator null vector of K satisfies Aw=Bw. This yields the stated mixed pairings on the form domain, whereas a scalar quadratic equality alone would not do so.

## 2. Exact convolution and exterior proof

With Theta(x)=exp(x/2) sum_n exp(-pi n^2 exp(2x)), differentiation gives (D^2-1/4)Theta=Phi. Its modular reflection gives Theta'(0)=-1/4. Thus .5exp(-|x|/2)-Theta(|x|) has neither a value nor derivative jump at zero and is the decaying solution of (-D^2+1/4)J=Phi. The Green kernel is exactly k1, proving the full convolution formula.

The source identity gives D_B=(C0-4)m^2-b_rem(Phi)/(2h), with C0>4. Each theta term is convex on x>=1 by the displayed polynomial in u=pi n^2 exp(2x). For x>=2 the symmetric-difference integral up to t=x-1 is nonnegative. On the rest, retaining Phi(x-t) on [x-1/4,x] and the entire negative diagonal gives the claimed lower bound. The integral tail of k_rem is at most .5 exp(-5(x-1)/2). The first theta term is >1/8 on [0,1/4]; the complete theta envelope at x>=2 is smaller than 70exp(-141). Consequently D_B>.01exp(-3|x|). The exact convolution and exterior constants also passed a separate analytic review.

## 3. Complete tails used by the implementation

For theta indices n>=4, monotonicity in nonnegative x bounds the Phi term by 64 n^4 exp(-3n^2). The complete sum is below 2^-40; the Theta tail is smaller. This applies also to the even evaluation at |x-log n|. The positive enclosure added by the script therefore includes the entire theta series.

For diagonal prime indices n>=128 and |x|<=2, both shifted Phi values are at most 41 n^(9/2) exp(-n^2/27). Multiplication by Lambda(n)/sqrt(n), bounded by sqrt(n), gives the all-integer majorant 82 sum n^5 exp(-n^2/27). Its first term and geometric ratio are respectively below 2^-558 and 1/256, and its complete sum is below 2^-100. The code adds that bound before dividing by 2h. The exterior lower bound and full theta envelope imply W<2^-80 outside [-2,2]. Together with the certified compact W^2<2, a pair with at least one exterior endpoint has product <2^-79. For n>=55>exp(4), one endpoint has absolute value at least .5log n. The monotone exterior envelope yields the stated all-integer tail 200 sum_(n>=55) n^3 4^-n <2^-70. ## 5. Passage back to the original operator

The complete relative shift series T converges absolutely in operator norm with ||T||<=r<4/5. The factorization Vp=D_B^(1/2) T D_B^(1/2) holds first on compact vectors and then on all original physical L2 by boundedness. It requires no bounded inverse of D_B. Hence D_B+Vp>=(1-r)D_B and likewise with the opposite sign. The k1 convolution is positive, so C>=0. The exact reducing ground vector then proves B>=0, with B[v]>=(1/5) integral D_B|v|^2 on e0-perp and the upper bound and kernel stated in (8).

This completes the original-domain proof and the numerical certificate. The distinct comparison A>=B remains open. No conclusion about its threshold is included in this approval.

## Review II. Weighted comparison and legal form-sandwich

## 1. Weighted coercivity without an unweighted gap

The exact remainder jump form is one half of the double integral Phi(x)Phi(y)k_rem(x-y)|q(x)-q(y)|^2. Its kernel dominates exp(-5|x|/2)exp(-5|y|/2), giving the weighted variance with weight w=Phi exp(-5|x|/2). On the original ground-perpendicular space, integral p q=0 and integral p=1. Weighted Cauchy gives |c|^2<=L integral w|q-c|^2, where L=integral p^2/w. This controls the uncentered weighted norm and yields exactly Z/[2(1+ZL)] integral exp(-3|x|)|v|^2.

All weighted means exist already for physical L2 vectors since w/p<=1/2. Z>1/48 follows from the complete first-theta lower bound on [-1/4,1/4], and Z<=1/2 follows from integral 2hPhi=1. The complete Mellin identity gives L<=8xi(4)=48zeta(4)/pi^2<8. Hence the coercivity coefficient exceeds 1/480 and therefore 2^-9. This uses no central cutoff or new test domain.

## 2. Complete weighted theta and Gamma bounds

The envelope Astar exp(9|x|/2-pi exp(2|x|)), Astar<64, proves M0<4 and sup exp(3|x|)m^2<4 by maximizing u^4exp(-3u). The Mellin identity gives M1<=2xi(3)=3zeta(3)/pi<3/2.

For the second derivative, the displayed theta polynomial has absolute value at most 74U^4 for U>=pi>3. The sum of n^8 exp(-pi(n^2-1)u) is <2 uniformly for u>=1, using the n=2 term and the stated geometric bound. Maximizing u^6exp(-3u) then gives M2<2^13. These estimates include every theta term and both half-lines.

For t<=1, k_rem(t)<=1/(2t), and the second difference costs t^2 exp(5/2)M2 after multiplication by exp(5|x|/2). Its integral is at most exp(5/2)M2/4. For t>=1 the diagonal costs at most M0 and the two off-diagonal integrals together at most (7/6)M1, since their transformed domains are disjoint. The total is <55301.75. The exact cancellation potential D_B=(C0-4)m^2-b_rem(Phi)/(2h), together with 0<C0-4<4, therefore obeys |D_B|<2^16 exp(-3|x|).

## 3. Entire relative prime series and upper comparison

Writing N=exp(3|x|/2)m, the k1 convolution has norm at most 4||N||_infinity^2<16 in the weighted physical norm. For each prime-power shift, the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives N(x)N(x+log n)<=Astar n^(7/2)exp(-pi n). The complete relative norm is bounded by 128 sum_(n>=2) n^4exp(-3n)<8. This is an all-integer majorant and includes every prime power; no absolute signed tail is divided by a vanishing weight.

Dropping only the nonpositive ground projection from B gives B<=2^17 rho as quadratic forms, rho=exp(-3|x|). Combining with the lower bound for A yields B<=2^26 A on H0. The separately certified B>=0 supplies the lower comparison. This upper comparison uses only analytic estimates.

## 4. Legal construction and exact threshold equivalence

On H0 the weighted lower bound implies ker A={0}. Thus Ran A^(1/2) is dense. The map T(A^(1/2)v)=B^(1/2)v is well-defined and bounded by 2^13 on that range. Its unique bounded extension gives Q=T*T, with 0<=Q<=2^26 I. Spectral calculus identifies D(A^(-1/2)) with Ran A^(1/2); no everywhere-defined unbounded product is assumed.

The form identity K[v]=<A^(1/2)v,(I-Q)A^(1/2)v> holds on exactly the original form domain. Density then proves equivalence of K>=0 and Q<=I. This does not supply the missing inequality Q<=I. Subtracting bounded B preserves the form and operator domains, rather than claiming B itself maps either domain into itself.

## 5. Infinite exact threshold-one family

The complete source operator has constant coefficients and sends Phi to -h. For every fixed j, complete-theta derivative estimates give f_j=D^(2j)(D^2-1/4)Phi and w_j=f_j/m in the original physical operator domain. The full prime series and its derivatives converge locally normally. Commutation with differentiation and (D^2-1/4)h=0 give H w_j=0. Integration by parts gives <e0,w_j>=2 integral h f_j=0, so K w_j=0.

For an original operator null, Aw=Bw. Pairing against all original form-domain vectors and using the dense range of A^(1/2) yields Q A^(1/2)w=A^(1/2)w. The vectors w_j are linearly independent: their Fourier transforms reduce a finite dependence to (t^2+1/4)P(t^2)Xi(t)=0, and Xi is nonzero near zero. A^(1/2) remains injective on H0, preserving independence. Thus Q has eigenvalue one of infinite multiplicity, is not compact, and has essential norm at least one.

This verifies the domain and structural consequences claimed by the manuscript. It does not assert any eigenvalue above one or a new negative spectral direction.
