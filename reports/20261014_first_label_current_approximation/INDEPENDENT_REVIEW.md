# Independent mathematical review

## Conclusion and precise scope

The theorem in `REPORT.md`, at the hash pinned below, is supported by the
complete proofs in this package. It constructs a legal original-form test
from the **first conditional hidden label of the same complete current**
and proves

\[
 F[w_1-u]\le \frac{13}{400}\mathcal E_{\mathrm{current}}.
\]

Here `F` has one future orientation. The original signed form is
`Q_delta[psi f] = 2 F[f] - B_delta[f]`. The bracket energy
`E_current` is not the positive safe-xi comparison component and is not
shown to be bounded above by `F[u]`.

The result is an energy approximation and a lawful stationarity test,
not an RH proof, a contraction of the full signed form, or an
all-label original-domain theorem.

## Independent checks

1. **The process and the field are the stated ones.** The full-band eta
   posterior, continuous channel, every Mangoldt divisor channel and
   genuine refresh channel are retained. The generator intertwining is
   an arbitrary-label identity. Its filtering statement is for the full
   observed history, and the origin extension retains the operational
   holding interval. The closed-current conditional mean is not replaced
   by a canonical latent quotient. Consequently the decomposition
   `J = V + R_G(T Gamma_A[w])` and the four nonnegative components
   `E_c, E_d, R_a, R_v` refer to one current and one lift.

2. **Original-domain closure is substantive.** The continuous
   conductance is exactly
   `psi(x) psi(y) kappa(y-x) p_n(y)`. The uniform positive first-label
   posterior floor pays its original Gamma energy. The conditional
   current bound pays the complete prime and pole rows. Origin
   flattening, theta-tail cutoff and annular logarithmic-form density
   establish membership in the original completed `P` domain. The
   signed adjoint-resolvent passage uses potentials dominated by `J`,
   with integrable absolute row pairing and vanishing cutoff errors;
   it does not assert positivity of the adjoint density `b`. Thus
   `E_current = integral b(J-V) = F[u] - integral b V` is valid without
   discarding the transport term.

3. **The moving posterior is paid, not frozen.** Splitting the first-label
   Gamma increment gives the stated hidden-increment and posterior-change
   terms. Monotonicity of every tail coordinate yields the complete
   chi-square estimate. The differentiation variable in the estimate
   `-epsilon' <= 4 epsilon` is physical `z = pi exp(2x)`.
   The exact change of variables gives the factor `1/(2 k(z))` in the
   posterior debit. The complete theta integral and rational exponential
   bounds yield respectively `epsilon < 1/400` and the debit `< 1/50`.
   This proves the two-term Gamma estimate with no truncated posterior.

4. **The prime comparison uses true divisors.** The complete theta-score
   bound `s' - 2s > 3` implies the stated dilation and rate comparison
   `L_n <= 4 a_n/3`. The prime-edge decomposition retains the actual
   hidden divisor square and the centered prelabel square. The exact
   posterior/conductance regrouping includes every prime power and gives
   the coefficients `3 epsilon_*`, `4 epsilon_*`, and `1/100` in the
   divisor/reset estimate. Its far posterior bound is
   `epsilon_* < 2^(-42)`.

5. **There is no duplicate energy expenditure.** Choosing `t = 12` in
   the Gamma estimate gives the four coefficients
   `13/400`, `3*2^(-42)`, `4*2^(-42)`, and `19/600` on
   `E_c, E_d, R_a, R_v`. Each is at most `13/400`. In particular the two
   contributions to `R_v` have been added before the comparison.
   The accompanying exact rational checker was independently rerun and
   passed; its role is to verify these explicit inequalities, not to
   replace the all-domain proofs.

6. **The signed consequence has the correct normalization.** Since
   `g0 = psi u` is a null representative of the same actual top source
   and `psi w_1` is legal, stationarity gives
   `Q_delta[psi w_1] = Q_delta[psi(w_1-u)]`.
   The resulting upper bound is precisely
   `13 E_current/200 - B_delta[w_1-u]`.
   The reflected row is reflected-only, the full pole has coefficient
   `-8`, and its changed moment under the plateau gauge is retained.
   Positivity of the boundary graph alone does not close this inequality.
