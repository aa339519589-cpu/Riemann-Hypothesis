# Full physical prime jump: compact critical complement and essential 1/2

Date: 2026-10-12. The target remains unconditional RH. This is an exact spectral statement for the unchanged full-theta, full-Gamma, all-prime generator. It concerns the physical prime jump in the original Markov form, NOT the artificial weighted comparison edge \(m(x)m(x+d)|v(x+d)-v(x)|^2\) used in previous no-go reports.

## 1. Definitions and inherited facts

Round 5 defines \(p=2\Phi h\), \(h=\cosh(x/2)\), \(m=\sqrt{\Phi/(2h)}\), \(Uq=\sqrt p\,q\), \(T=ULU^{-1}=\frac12 I+H\), and
\[
H=B-V,\quad B=M_m b(D)M_m\ge0,\quad V=C_0M_{m^2}+S_m.
\]
The true weighted prime shifts
\(S_N=\sum_{2\le n\le N}\alpha_nM_m(\tau_{\log n}+\tau_{-\log n})M_m\), with \(\alpha_n=\Lambda(n)/\sqrt n\), converge in operator norm to bounded \(S_m\). The critical space \(\mathcal K=\ker H\) is infinite-dimensional by the exact modes \((\Phi^{(2r)}-4^{-r}\Phi)/m\).

The original physical prime-jump energy in \(L^2(\nu)\) is
\[
E_{P,N}(q)=\sum_{2\le n\le N}\alpha_n
\int\Phi(x)\Phi(x+\log n)|q(x+\log n)-q(x)|^2dx.
\tag{1}
\]
Its bounded, positive representative in the v=Uq variable is
\[
J_N=M_{\lambda_N}-S_N,\qquad
\lambda_N(x)=\frac1{2h(x)}
\sum_{2\le n\le N}\alpha_n[\Phi(x+\log n)+\Phi(x-\log n)].
\tag{2}
\]
The full prime operator is \(J=M_{\lambda_P}-S_m=\mathrm{s}\!-\!\lim_N J_N\), where the original exact source identity yields
\[
\lambda_P=\frac{S\Phi}{2h}=\tfrac12+\frac{A\Phi}{2h},\qquad
\lambda_P(x)\to\tfrac12,\quad
\lambda_N(x)\to0\ \text{for each finite }N
\quad (|x|\to\infty).
\tag{3}
\]
The boundedness of \(\lambda_P\) and these tails are proved in round 5, §§4,6. The Archimedean generator \(A_{\rm arch}=T-J\ge0\) on Dom T is exactly the continuous strictly positive squared-difference form.

## 2. Compactness lemma

**Lemma.** If \(W\in L^\infty(\mathbb R)\) and \(W(x)\to0\) at both spatial tails, then \(M_W|_{\mathcal K}:\mathcal K\to L^2\) is compact. Also \(S_m|_{\mathcal K}\) and every \(S_N|_{\mathcal K}\) are compact.

**Proof.** For \(\|u\|_2\le1,u\in\mathcal K\), \(Bu=Vu\). Set \(g=mu\). Then
\[
\int b(\xi)|\widehat g(\xi)|^2\frac{d\xi}{2\pi}
=\langle u,Bu\rangle=\langle u,Vu\rangle\le\|V\|.
\tag{4}
\]
The real symbol \(b(\xi)\to\infty\), since its exact nonnegative sum contains \(\frac12\sum_{j+1/4\le|\xi|/2}(j+1/4)^{-1}\). This bounds the high-frequency tails of g uniformly. Its spatial tails are uniformly small because m vanishes at infinity. Kolmogorov-Riesz makes the family g precompact in \(L^2\). On each compact x interval, division by the smooth positive m preserves local precompactness of u. Spatial cutoff and \(\sup_{|x|>R}|W(x)|\to0\) yield compactness of \(M_W|_{\mathcal K}\).

Each \(M_m\tau_{\pm\log n}M_m|_{\mathcal K}\) factors through the compact \(M_m|_{\mathcal K}\). Thus the finite S_N restrictions are compact and their norm limit \(S_m|_{\mathcal K}\) is compact. \(\square\)

## 3. Spectral theorem for all physical prime jumps on the true nullspace

Let P be the orthogonal projection onto \(\mathcal K\). Since \(Tu=\frac12u\) for every \(u\in\mathcal K\), the compressed full Archimedean operator is
\[
C_A=P A_{\rm arch}|_{\mathcal K}
=P[M_{1/2-\lambda_P}+S_m]|_{\mathcal K}.
\tag{5}
\]

**Theorem.** \(C_A\) is compact, positive, and injective on infinite-dimensional \(\mathcal K\), with eigenvalues \(\mu_j\downarrow0\) satisfying \(0<\mu_j<1/2\). Moreover
\[
\boxed{P J|_{\mathcal K}=\tfrac12 I_{\mathcal K}-C_A,\qquad
\sigma_{\rm ess}(P J|_{\mathcal K})=\{\tfrac12\}.}
\tag{6}
\]
The full physical prime compression has eigenvalues \(1/2-\mu_j\in(0,1/2)\) tending to the **non-eigenvalue** essential threshold 1/2. For any fixed finite N, \(P J_N|_{\mathcal K}\) is compact, and
\[
\boxed{\sigma_{\rm ess}(P(J-J_N)|_{\mathcal K})=\{\tfrac12\},\qquad
\|P(J-J_N)|_{\mathcal K}\|_{\rm ess}=\tfrac12.}
\tag{7}
\]
Thus finite-prime compressions converge strongly but not in norm to the complete prime compression, even after restricting to the genuine critical space.

**Proof.** The multiplier \(1/2-\lambda_P\) vanishes at infinity by (3); both terms in (5) are compact by the lemma. The form of \(A_{\rm arch}\) is
\[
\frac12\iint\Phi(x)\Phi(y)k(x-y)|q(x)-q(y)|^2dxdy,
\quad q=U^{-1}u,\quad k(s)>0\ (s\ne0).
\]
Zero Archimedean energy forces q to be constant a.e. A vector \(u\in\mathcal K\) corresponds to an eigenvector of T with eigenvalue 1/2, hence is orthogonal to the ground state \(U1\), which has eigenvalue 0; a nonzero constant q is impossible. Therefore \(C_A\) is positive injective.

The full physical prime energy is likewise strictly positive on \(\mathcal K\). If it vanished, its n=2 and n=3 terms would make q invariant a.e. under both \(\log2\) and \(\log3\). Their ratio is irrational; local mollification followed by continuity under the dense subgroup generated by these periods forces q to be constant a.e., again excluded. Hence \(PJ|_{\mathcal K}>0\) in the injective-form sense. Identity (6) follows from \(A_{\rm arch}=T-J\) on K. Thus \(0<C_A<\frac12I\) in the strict quadratic-form sense; the spectral theorem for compact positive injective operators gives \(0<\mu_j<1/2\), \(\mu_j\to0\), and a complete eigenbasis. This proves (6).

For finite N, \(\lambda_N\) vanishes at infinity, and S_N restricts compactly. Thus J_N restricts compactly by the lemma. The tail compression is \(\frac12I\) plus a compact operator, yielding (7) by Weyl's theorem and the Calkin essential norm. Strong convergence comes from the bounded increasing positive prime forms; norm convergence is excluded by (7). \(\square\)

## 4. Exact critical vectors witness the missing half-energy

The already proved theta translations
\[
v_t(x)=\frac{\Phi(x+t)+\Phi(x-t)-2\cosh(t/2)\Phi(x)}{m(x)},
\quad 0<t<(\log2)/2,
\]
are nonzero elements of \(\mathcal K\). Their normalized versions \(u_t=v_t/\|v_t\|\) converge weakly to zero and escape every spatial compact as \(t\uparrow(\log2)/2\).

**Corollary.** For every fixed finite N,
\[
\boxed{
\|J_Nu_t\|\to0,\quad
\|(J-\tfrac12I)u_t\|\to0,\quad
\|(J-J_N-\tfrac12I)u_t\|\to0,\quad
\|A_{\rm arch}u_t\|\to0.
}
\tag{8}
\]
Thus for \(q_t=U^{-1}u_t\), the complete jump energies satisfy
\[
E_{P,N}(q_t)\to0,\qquad
E_P(q_t)\to\tfrac12,\qquad
E_{\rm arch}(q_t)\to0,\qquad
(E_P-E_{P,N})(q_t)\to\tfrac12 .
\tag{9}
\]

**Proof.** Multipliers \(\lambda_N,\lambda_P-1/2\) have the stated limits at infinity, so multiplying the escaping u_t makes them vanish strongly. The compact operators \(S_N|_{\mathcal K},S_m|_{\mathcal K}\) take the weakly-null u_t to strongly-null vectors. Substitution in J_N and J gives (8), using \(Tu_t=\frac12u_t\) for the Arch term. Inner products prove (9). \(\square\)

## 5. What this does and does not establish

The complete prime power sum is noncompact on the true infinite critical space although every fixed finite cutoff is compact there. Its essential spectral threshold is **exactly** 1/2; true critical eigenvectors give explicit Weyl sequences for the omitted prime energy. This is a structural nonuniformity theorem that any full-RH argument using finite primes must confront.

No positivity of H on \(\mathcal K^\perp\), exclusion of off-line zeros, or RH is proved. The inherited source/domain identification and tail asymptotics still require independent review; no formal checker is claimed. No existing file is overwritten.
