Date: 2026-10-16.

The complete written proof in [the addendum](RECIPROCAL_INTEGRABILITY_ADDENDUM.md)

The following points were verified.

1. The hypotheses require the complete ground identities and lawful
   one-sided minimality. Normalizing `h+t psi` preserves the ground energy,
   so the selected fixed-source or joint minimum supplies the latter
   premise without a fixed-ground gauge derivative.
2. The exact edge quotient, its subtracted-square completion, and the
   elementary positive-part bound for the forward rows have the stated
   factors. At a common-zero endpoint the quotient is interpreted by the
   stated zero convention; a single such endpoint gives exactly the
   finite expression written in Section 2.
3. The complete pole quotient is `ell(q_t)-8 J_t`, using `ell(h)=1` and
   `ell(psi)=1/8`. Its negative term is essential to the absorption and
   is not omitted or replaced by an independently assumed budget.
4. The variable-cut Gamma argument uses the union of the two directed
   near sets. Its complement satisfies both cutoffs, which licenses the
   far-set symmetrization. The near estimate uses Holder continuity and
   `integral psi/s<infinity`; its constant can depend on the chosen cutoff
   parameter but is independent of the mixture parameter `t`.
5. The far Gamma row and the entire forward-prime row, including proper
   powers, are paid by the stated Cauchy--Schwarz and Young estimates.
   The needed fixed theta integrals are finite both at the wall and at
   infinity. No regularity or positive lower bound for the rough ground
   is introduced.
6. At every fixed positive `t`, `J_t<=ell(q)/t`, and both complete
   positive energies are finite. Thus the integrated finite differences
   are legitimate before the uniform estimate is proved. One-sided
   minimality and the retained pole coefficient give the uniform bound;
   no derivative at zero is used.
7. Monotone convergence gives the reciprocal square in (17). The
   displayed theta-weight comparisons then give every fixed exponential
   L2 and L1 conclusion for `q=f^2/h`, with the common-zero convention
   retained throughout.

The estimate resolves reciprocal-wall integrability at the selected
minimum. It does not establish strict positivity, lower comparison with
psi, finite fixed-ground gauge energy, feasible signed neutral
variations, a representation of the positive neutral cone, or the
original sharp spectral/arithmetic comparison. The review is an
independent internal mathematical check, not external certification or
an RH proof.
