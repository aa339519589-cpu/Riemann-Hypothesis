# Opposite auxiliary orientation: the complete positive Kummer block

Choose xi=xi0 chi_b in the same physical half-damped construction. Its actual Kummer coordinate becomes [m/c^2], rather than [m/s^2]. For this new observation, the full positive within-class energy has square root

    (sum_gamma sum_h |normalized restored I_(b,gamma,h)|^2)^(1/2)
        << Z^(-23/96+epsilon).

The assertion includes every physical n and s, original/core nonflat marks, every actual finite-compensation subset and the stated Gaussian-T operator. It is uniform for each genuine auxiliary b and then for the positive ensemble average. No Q is used. This positive quantity is below the desired Z^(-3/16) scale by 5/96. The cross-class signed sum is still unestimated; the high comparison for this changed auxiliary orientation has not been established here. The previously published fixed-signal high theorem is not automatically transferred.

## 1. Exact physical phase for the new admissible choice

Fix eta=1 and the original base data S0,b0,xi0. For the same squarefree auxiliary product b choose

             xi=xi0 chi_b,

where each local chi_p is the original primitive sextic character. It is a permitted nonprincipal order-dividing-six local auxiliary. Keep its full normalized primitive Gauss root, all fixed ray factors and every original zero mask. Put A=c n^3 as in the original physical definition.

The exact change from the base physical coefficient is

 epsilon_b R(A/s,b) chi_b(m A^(-2)),
 epsilon_b=tau_(chi_b)^(-1) conjugate(xi0(b)) conjugate(chi_b(b0)),
                                                          (1)

on (b,A s m)=1. All powers in (1) are character expressions on actual units. To verify it, the full CRT root is

 tau_(xi0 chi_b)=tau0 tau_(chi_b) xi0(b) chi_b(b0).

The changes in xi(m)/xi(s), chi_s(b*) and Xi(A)^(-1) are respectively

 chi_b(m/s),  R(s,b)^(-1)chi_b(s),
 R(A,b)chi_b(A)^(-2).

Their product is (1). Since A^(-2)=c^(-2)n^(-6), its Kummer label is exactly

                  [m/c^2] in K^times/K^(times 6).        (2)

It is not the previous orientation's label [m/s^2]. Original unit classes are retained. The original coefficient chi_A(m), including (A,m)=1, is indispensable here.

First retain the same finite original ray-sector partition gamma=A/s used in the reviewed physical second-moment identity. Let I_(b,gamma,h) be the full physical contribution in that sector with label (2) equal to h. Within one gamma, the reciprocal R factor is a common auxiliary scalar, so the principal pair blocks are precisely these h classes. Define N_b=(sum_gamma sum_h|I_(b,gamma,h)|^2)^(1/2), for the finite-compensated half-damped observation. The finite positive sector reduction costs only the original fixed number of sectors. Every estimate below is uniform with its sector indicator retained: for fixed c,n it is a bounded restriction on the s-row coefficients. We abbreviate a fixed sector by I_(b,h) during the proof, and restore the finite sector sum at the end.

## 2. Statement and actual scales

Keep the original geometry

 X=Z^(17/48), Y=Z^(23/48), ell=1/6, 0<T<=Z,

and the original fixed number of disjoint prime slots. In a compensation summand D=p_J is rescaled, E=p_(J^c) is marked, with coefficient (ND)^(-3/2), scales X/ND,Y/ND,T NE, and the exact original or core divisibility test. Use the same auxiliary product b at every T and every summand; B=N(b0 b).

The conclusion is

 N_b <<_epsilon ambient^epsilon
                 Y^(-1/2)[1+Y(BXY)^(-1/3)]              (3)

for the ENTIRE physical s support, all n, all fixed-slot summands and their true common Kummer-class space. Here ambient may be BZ and constants depend only on the fixed profiles, base data and slot system. The original c_Sb^(-1) and A_T(Z)^(-1) normalization, followed by the OLD Gaussian-T restoring operator at b, obey the same estimate. For the actual pool R0=Z^128, every Nb>=R0^(64/65-o(1)); hence

        (sum_gamma sum_h |normalized restored I_(b,gamma,h)|^2)^(1/2)
                         <<_epsilon Z^(-23/96+epsilon). (4)

The bound is uniform in b, so also bounds the square root of its positive ensemble mean. It requires no Q. It is stronger than the desired Z^(-3/16) scale by 5/96 only for this positive within-class quantity. We make no assertion that this controls the whole physical second moment or its signed first moment.

## 3. First the squarefree-s core: c, rather than s, is encoded in h

For now s is squarefree. Its primitive Gauss formula is

 g_(chi_s)(s,-m)/sqrt(Ns)=gamma1(s)conjugate(chi_s(-m)).

The original completed coefficient enforces (c,m)=1. Since c is squarefree, choosing the sixth-power-free representative h of [m/c^2] gives exactly

       c | h4,       h=c^4 d,       m=d z^6,
                    (c,dz)=1.                           (5)

Thus, for a fixed h, c has only divisor-many possibilities. The m window also gives Nh << B X'Y' (Nc)^4 in each actual summand. Cauchy in the finite c choices costs only a small power, which is retained as an ambient/c Gaussian moment. No false factorization of chi_A(h) across primes shared with c is used.

For fixed c,n,z, the remaining s-character in the d column is conjugate(chi_s(d)). Indeed chi_A(d) and xi(d) are factors independent of s, and can be removed from the positive d norm only after c,n have been fixed. The restrictions (A,d)=1 and (A,z)=1 remain genuine common/output or row masks. The condition (s,dz)=1 is retained by chi_s(d) and a row restriction. All finite phases, including R(A/s,b), are bounded s-row coefficients. The primitive s family has genuine conductor O(Ns), bounded multiplicity after the fixed finite ray partition.

## 4. The changing radial s window is kept, not frozen

Freeze X',Y' in one summand and write M0=B X'Y'. The actual radial weight is

 (B Ns X')^(-1/2) W0(Nd (Nz)^6/(B Ns X')).

Because Ns/Y' stays in a fixed compact interval, its support puts Nd in a fixed annulus about D_z=M0/(Nz)^6. Insert a common smooth d cutoff equal to one on that support. Mellin inversion in the ratio Ns/Y' separates W0, with an integrable fixed profile; (Ns/Y')^(-1/2) is a bounded row coefficient. Thus no B-dependent or z-dependent seminorm enters the row large sieve.

Use the reviewed all-primitive Hecke conductor large sieve and duality for the s rows:

 sum_(Nd<<D) |sum_(Ns~Y',s sf) a_s conjugate(chi_s(d))|^2
       <<(D+(Y')^2)(DY')^epsilon sum_s|a_s|^2.           (6)

The actual s coefficient includes Y'^(-1) W1(Ns/Y'), normalized gamma1(s), the separated norm mode and bounded phases. Hence its squared mass is O(1/Y'). Extra zero masks only restrict that positive mass. Formula (6) is valid for arbitrary such common coefficients; no fixed-order index is replaced by a conductor bound.

If Y' or D_z lies below one, a nonempty actual compact support forces it to be bounded below by a fixed positive constant. Clamp these parameters to one before applying (6), at only a fixed constant cost; empty supports contribute zero. This also applies after the higher-s reductions of Section 6.

Minkowski in the common Mellin variable and then in z, preserving (c n,z)=1, gives for fixed c,n, apart from its outside c,n/Gaussian coefficient,

 norm_(ell2(d)) << ambient^epsilon
            (Y')^(-1/2)[1+Y' M0^(-1/3)].                (7)

Precisely, the sqrt(D_z) term sums to sqrt(M0) sum_z (Nz)^(-3), and the Y' term has O(M0^(1/6)) possible z. Dividing by the true M0^(1/2) normalization proves (7). This accounts for every sixth-power column and the full changing B window.

## 5. Marked c primes must be grouped before taking an absolute slot sum

Fix which slots are rescaled, and fix their actual tuple D. For each marked slot classify its prime as a c mark when it divides c, and as an n mark otherwise. Write E=Ec En. This is a disjoint partition of the actual marked support, because E|c n^3 implies En|n. In the core version its genuine n condition may only be more restrictive. Retain the restrictions that En is coprime to c and every original slot prime is distinct.

Fix En and its assignment for the moment. For one h, every possible Ec divides c and hence h4 by (5). The combined choices of c and the marked-c tuple Ec therefore have divisor-small multiplicity, even though their primes range over the original growing slot pools. Apply Cauchy to these choices TOGETHER, leaving squared weights prod W_i(P)^2 for Ec. This is where an unrestricted l1 sum of marked c slots would unnecessarily lose Z^(ell/2).

After this Cauchy and (7), apply Minkowski only in n. Its true half-damped factor is (Nn)^(-3/2). On En|n write n=En v; discarding other restrictions only in the positive majorant gives

 sum_(En|n) (Nn)^(-3/2)
               V_G(Nc(Nn)^3/(T NEc NEn))
 <=(NEn)^(-3/2) G(T NEc/(Nc(NEn)^2)),                   (8)

where G(U)=sum_v (Nv)^(-3/2)V_G((Nv)^3/U), retaining the original log-Gaussian. Ideal counting gives

 G(U)<< U^(-1/6) for U>=1,
 G(U)<<_N U^N for 0<U<1.                                (9)

Now c is a multiple of Ec. Its square coefficient and the complete Gaussian moment satisfy, uniformly for all T>0,

 sum_(Ec|c,c sf) (Nc)^(-1)
                 G(T NEc/(Nc(NEn)^2))^2
                         << (NEc)^(-1).                (10)

Set c=Ec c0. On dyads Nc0~C, the harmonic c0 mass is bounded and (9) gives a summable sequence both above and below C=T/(NEn)^2. This proves (10), including T/(NEn)^2<1; no artificial c or n cutoff has been inserted.

The marked-c sum is now

       sum_Ec prod_(p|Ec) W_i(P)^2 / NEc << 1            (11)

for the fixed number of slots, by the elementary ideal/prime count sum_(p~P_i)1/P<<1. Small-power losses from the divisor Cauchy are absorbed by the same Gaussian moments with a smaller preliminary epsilon.

Equations (8)-(11) therefore pay every c-marked slot without a positive power of its scale. A fixed En costs (NEn)^(-3/2), and its outer l1 slot sum satisfies sum_(p~P_i)W_i(P)P^(-3/2)<<1. Rescaled slots have the true factor

        (ND)^(-3/2)(Y/ND)^(-1/2)=Y^(-1/2)(ND)^(-1),

whose outer slot sums are also O(1). There are only finitely many assignments of the fixed slot system. The remaining bracket in (7) is

 1+(Y/ND)(BXY/(ND)^2)^(-1/3)
       =1+Y(BXY)^(-1/3)(ND)^(-1/3),                    (12)

which only improves under rescaling. This proves (3) for squarefree physical s with all actual compensation terms included.

The Gaussian completed scale in (8) is T NEc NEn, not T. Its exact cancellation of NEc after c=Ec c0, and the remaining (NEn)^2 in (8), are essential. The proof is not a finite-slot redefinition or a coefficientwise claim that marked and rescaled states cancel.

## 6. Every higher s valuation and the original class coordinate

Write s=s1 t with s1 squarefree, t powerful and (s1,t)=1. Every prime p|t is prime to A on the original support. The reviewed exact complete-m reductions apply at each fixed compensation summand:

 - if k=v_p(t) is not divisible by six, the branch is P^(-k) times a column with primitive auxiliary xi chi_p^(-k), Y divided by P^k, and X,T unchanged;
 - if 6|k, it is P^(-k) times the two complete columns at X and PX, with weights 1 and -P^(-1/2)omega_p(A,s1), where omega_p=conjugate(xi(p))chi_s1(p)conjugate(chi_A(p)).

The old phase label is now [m/c^2]. In the first case it is the new label multiplied by [p^(k-1)]. In the principal case its first/bulk label is unchanged and its second label is multiplied by [p^(-1)]. These are fixed bijections on the class space. Their scalar and A/s row phases are independent of d,z after the indicated fixed c,n step; they are legal arbitrary coefficients in (6).

The temporary auxiliary modulus B_t is at least B, and the temporary X_t is at least X. Every nonzero branch is coprime to the marked ideal E at the primes of t. The marked-c divisors are therefore still encoded in h4 after a class permutation: those permutations involve only primes outside c. The complete CRT phases and all new true holes are retained; no new restoring operator or c_S normalization is added at these temporary auxiliary primes.

For fixed t, the common outside coefficient is (Nt)^(-1), and Y changes to Y/Nt. The total triangle weight of principal high-valuation choices is

 C(t)=prod_(p|t,6|v_p(t))(1+P^(-1/2)) <<_epsilon (Nt)^epsilon.

The preceding compensated squarefree-s proof then bounds its contribution by

 ambient^epsilon Y^(-1/2)(Nt)^(-1/2)C(t)
       [1+Y(BXY)^(-1/3)(Nt)^(-2/3)].                  (13)

Keeping B_t or X_t would improve the bracket. The compact original s window forces Nt<<Y/ND<=O(Y). Powerful ideal counting gives

 sum_(Nt<<Y,t powerful) (Nt)^(-1/2+epsilon)<<Y^(2epsilon),
 sum_(t powerful) (Nt)^(-7/6+epsilon)<<1.

Minkowski with (13) proves (3) for ALL original physical s. The k=5 invisible layer, all Ramanujan boundaries/bulk, the possible p|c,s1 shared states, and all n are included.

## 7. Normalization and the full Gaussian restorer

The original c_Sb^(-1) is uniformly O(1) on this same pool, and A_T(Z)^(-1) costs a fixed logarithmic power. Neither has been replaced by its value at B_t. The old restoring operator at b changes only T, which does not occur in the bound (3). Its norm on this class-valued sup_(0<T<=Z) space is bounded by

 prod_(p|b)(1+O(P^(-5/6)))=O(1),

with its real geometric series P^(-3j) and the same original class label. This justifies (4) for the actual restored observation, using a smaller initial epsilon to pay B<=Z^128.

The estimate is already uniform for each actual b, so a positive without-replacement average adds no factor. No independence of choices, prime-Q cancellation or change of the physical m scale was used.

## 8. What is still unproved

A second moment of this new admissible orientation decomposes into the positive blocks estimated in (4) and a signed cross-class sum. The latter has not been bounded by (3)-(4). Applying a pointwise prime-Q estimate to it followed by an unrestricted m-column l1 sum still loses the growing auxiliary modulus. Thus (4) alone is NOT a low bound for the whole new observation and yields no RH conclusion.

The opposite high auxiliary orientation is also a different observation. At the source high side its character is conjugate(chi_b(u)); formal conjugation of a prime average suggests the same high projection, but no such high assertion is needed or claimed in this low proof. Any eventual full application must verify the exact deleted local factors, c_Sb and old Gaussian restorer for this orientation too.
