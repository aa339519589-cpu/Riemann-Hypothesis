# Provenance correction

**16 October 2026.** The central obstruction in the accompanying report was
already established in teal-sea/zeta-lab's
[Third-moment obstruction, Sections 1–2](https://github.com/teal-sea/zeta-lab/blob/main/hunts/cycle_moments/THIRD-MOMENT-OBSTRUCTION.md).
That note constructs admissible signed-pair configurations whose second and
third moments approach prescribed values with negative cubic discrepancy,
while their simple-count ratio approaches the second-moment baseline.

The overlap is exact, not merely conceptual. Its negative block is

    diag(b+2, b+2, -2b) = E_-(b+1).

Thus both that block and the asymptotic negative-discrepancy obstruction must
be attributed to the linked source. No novelty of the central obstruction
is claimed here.

The accompanying report supplies a finite allocation with traces exactly
N, CN, DN for every sufficiently large integer N, and treats either sign of
the cubic discrepancy. These are distinctions in the stated formulation,
not a claim of established novelty for the extension. Neither construction
realizes actual zeta zeros.
