## Limitations essential to the conclusion

A negative actual Schur value survives the mass neutral projection. The
explicit nontrivial complement is not signed by the reciprocal transform.
Finite favorable upper Gram values alone do not certify the limiting sign;
the complete residual must be bounded. None of the topology changes supplies
an original-P inverse, a hidden-energy orthogonal projection, or a new
positive budget for the remaining original signed form.
