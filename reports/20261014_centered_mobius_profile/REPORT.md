## 1. Exact setting and main theorem

u=r-c_infinity, its full eta current w, and the complete posterior p_n.
Thus T w=u, H_n=w_n-u, T H=0, and V=T|H|^2 is the SAME conditional

    a(n)=1-mu_ar(n), m=T a, M=T a^2, sigma^2=M-m^2,
    A_*=T(aH)/sigma^2,

Then T D=T Z=T(aZ)=0 and exactly

    V=sigma^2|A_*|^2+T|Z|^2.

The field D is the conditional orthogonal Mobius projection of the actual
current. It is NOT the canonical future inverse M(g-c psi). Its original
averaged form domain is established, rather than inferred from bounded
amplitude or posterior summability alone.

Let E_sp be the genuine hidden continuous-Gamma plus divisor energy,
without refresh. Let F_star be the full original forward energy of the
ONE coherent star-label field, with its honest pair law. Then

    E_sp[D]>=F_star[D]+(9/5)int_0^infinity psi^2 M|A_*|^2. (1)

The comparison retains the full signed moving-centering kernel. The
negative continuous cross is paid by a complete Schur bound; the prime

No derivative energy, reset square or previous hub budget is spent on
those scalar Schur debits.

For the COMPLETE original Gamma-wall/reflected-prime/pole form B_delta,
let B_D be its evaluation on the SAME centered covariance

   =A_*(x)conj(A_*(y))[M(max(x,y))-m(x)m(y)].

this gives the full boundary block

    2E_sp[D]-B_D>=(18/5)int psi^2 M|A_*|^2.             (2)

The negative pole is kept inside B_D. This is one signed arithmetic block,
not a second independent use of a current or boundary energy.

## 3. The exact remaining full-current comparison

The orthogonal residual in Section1 is not deleted or identified with a
new independent budget. At different spatial points D and Z need not be
orthogonal under the star law. The direct complete boundary theorem gives

    |B_DZ|<=(101/190)sqrt(B_D B_Z),
    (89/190)(B_D+B_Z)<=B_H<=(291/190)(B_D+B_Z).

This controls the mixed B block but not the remaining spatial/residual
block. Their complete forms satisfy exactly

 E_sp[H]=E_sp[D]+E_sp[Z]+2Re E_sp(D,Z),
 B_H=B_D+B_Z+2Re B_DZ,

 2E_sp[H]-B_H
   =(2E_sp[D]-B_D)+(2E_sp[Z]-B_Z)
                          +2Re[2E_sp(D,Z)-B_DZ].             (3)

Here every B includes the original Gamma wall, all reflected prime powers
and the full pole covariance, with the SAME joint star field. Equation(2)
signs only the first term of (3). The remaining residual block and the
whole mixed term in (3) must still be controlled jointly. Pointwise
orthogonality T(D conj Z)=0 does not make the off-site cross vanish.
In the real-field convention of the exact residual interface (with the
complex version obtained by Hermitian polarization), r=-T[c(a-m)Z] and

    E_sp(D,Z)-F_star(D,Z)=-int psi^2 A_* r+C_mix-I_div,
    E_reset(D,Z)=-int psi^2 A_* r.

RESIDUAL_CROSS.md defines the full C_mix and I_div, with both triangular
terms and every true divisor, and proves that these are the SAME r, not
independent errors. Thus adding refresh produces coefficient-2 for its
local residual rather than an extra sign gain.

The lawful scalar source h=T[a(w-u)]=sigma^2 A_* obeys
Q_delta(psi u,psi h)=0 by the bounded-mask theorem. Neither psi A_* nor

No actual-null condition is transferred to h, D, Z or w_1, and none of
these actual-input bounds may be iterated on such a new input.

The separate Gaussian-removed all-prime reset principal is deliberately
not part of this package: its metric and forcing comparison cannot replace
