## Checks

1. **Good endpoints and contour.** The stated local logarithmic-derivative formula was checked directly in Elkies' primary author notes, printed page 3, formula (6). Its uniform real strip contains the proposed contour for all sufficiently large T. The local zero count and the excluded intervals supply the good heights and the horizontal bound. The positively oriented rectangle gives a negative right-Euler term. On the downward left side the functional equation gives the positive Gamma term and the nonresonant Euler branch. The Gamma derivative error integrates to O(1); no pole at height zero is enclosed.
2. **Complete tails.** Chebyshev partial summation pays the omitted right-prime tail uniformly for d at most lambda, with a constant depending on mu minus lambda. The other Euler branch has no positive-frequency resonance. The absolute kernel estimates (C4) pay every product containing an error and every good-endpoint correction after the natural exponential factors are combined. The resulting squared error is o(T log T) for each fixed lambda less than one.
3. **Prime–prime composition.** The full-line extension retains the compact middle window and pays both causal collars. In (C5), the Lipschitz cancellation controls the compact region, while outside a fixed interval both Dirichlet kernels decay and their product is integrable. This is a uniform estimate in both prime labels. The projection identity supplies the factor 2 pi/L. Products above X are nonresonant and paid separately. Exponential conjugation is removed only after composition; its complete squared error is o(T log T), since mu is fixed below one.
4. **Prime–prime main term.** Plancherel contributes 2 pi/L and the preceding convolution contributes the additional L^-2 in the squared norm. The elementary interval mean-square estimate is uniform in the height endpoints and in the coefficients. The convolution coefficient square sum is O(log^4 X), including the same-prime-base terms. These factors give precisely O(T log T), not an omitted logarithmic factor.
5. **Both mixed compositions.** For each order, extracting the common natural exponential produces the causal Gamma kernel with the doubled damping. Freezing the middle window at the prime resonance gives respectively the stated s-dependent or t-dependent coefficient. The Lipschitz error is paid by the complete L1 norm of the damped Gamma kernel. The exact convolution then uses the regularized multiplier H, not an unregularized endpoint Hilbert transform.
6. **Gamma multiplier payment.** Its Fourier normalization and derivative signs were independently checked. Its endpoint logarithm is correctly capped by the strictly positive regularization. The O(log T) variation pays the conjugation error. Dyadic endpoint collars and the complete elementary Dirichlet mean square give (C10). Since X is a fixed power less than T, the extra X times logarithmic factors are lower order. Fixing the appropriate outer variable makes the variable coefficient legitimate in each Plancherel step.
7. **Gamma–Gamma.** Removing its decaying exponential costs only O(L) pointwise, and the full product error is negligible. The untriangular localized Gamma operator has bounded operator norm and squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm O(TL), hence fourth Schatten norm to the fourth power O(TL). Arazy's primary paper, printed page 299, explicitly states boundedness of triangular projection on Schatten p for 1<p<infinity and identifies its background reference. The report now gives the required interval-step compression passage: convergence is in Hilbert–Schmidt norm, the shrinking diagonal blocks vanish, and the uniform finite-dimensional S4 bound passes to the causal limit.
8. **Order of limits.** The leading main-term bounds use only a uniform bound for the window, a frequency interval of length at most one and the fixed comparable height endpoints. Window derivatives and the separation mu-lambda enter lower-order errors at each fixed window. Thus the stated supremum of individual leading limsups is supported. This does not justify a uniform starting height, interchange of supremum and limit, or a width depending on T.

## Finding

No mathematical gap was found in the pinned argument for its stated causal-square conclusion. The proof avoids a general sharp Fourier-multiplier assertion for the full zero operator and does not derive spectral fourth-moment control from this weaker observable. A sharp cubic transfer, mixed shrinking-height estimates and any count-improvement argument remain separate obligations; none is accepted merely by this review.
