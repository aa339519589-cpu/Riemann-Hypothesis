## R9-EH2-01 — PRIME/THETA SOURCE-DEFORMATION TANGENT

### I. 成立性裁决

**Verdict: 成立（在报告明确限定的 ordinary trivial-character Tate–Bruhat–Schwartz / regular normal-limit category 内）。**

The returned headline was:

> C. EVERY NATURAL REGULAR SOURCE DEFORMATION IN THE DEFINED CATEGORY IS EITHER PRINCIPAL-IDEAL / TRIVIAL OR GENERIC AND NON-RIGID.

The precise theorem stated at the end is correct in the defined category. The wording “every natural” must not be exported beyond that category.

#### A. 前提与定义

Use the trivial-character Tate zeta integral over Q. At finite p, normalize additive Haar measure by vol(Z_p)=1 and multiplicative Haar measure so vol(Z_p^×)=1. At infinity use d×x=dx/|x|.

Let

\[
g(x)=e^{-\pi x^2},\qquad E=x\frac d{dx},
\]

and

\[
h=\frac12E(E+1)g=(2\pi^2x^4-3\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2}.
\]

**Fact.** Integration by parts gives

\[
Z_\infty(Ef,s)=-sZ_\infty(f,s).
\]

Hence

\[
Z_\infty(h,s)=\frac12s(s-1)\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s),
\qquad
\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s)=\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2).
\]

**Fact.** Fourier transform satisfies

\[
\widehat{Ef}=-(E+1)\widehat f,
\]

so E(E+1) is Fourier invariant. Since g is self-dual, h is self-dual. Also h(0)=0 and, by self-duality, \(\widehat h(0)=0\). Thus the usual Tate endpoint pole terms vanish for the completed source.

At each finite p,

\[
Z_p(1_{\mathbb Z_p},s)=(1-p^{-s})^{-1}.
\]

Therefore the restricted tensor

\[
\phi_\xi=h\otimes\bigotimes_p1_{\mathbb Z_p}
\]

has

\[
Z(\phi_\xi,s)=\xi(s).
\]

This normalization chain is correct.

#### B. Finite-place local divisibility

Let \(\psi_p\in\mathcal S(\mathbb Q_p)\). Write

\[
a_k=\int_{\mathbb Z_p^\times}\psi_p(p^ku)\,d^\times u.
\]

Compact support gives \(a_k=0\) for all sufficiently negative k. Local constancy at 0 gives \(a_k=\psi_p(0)\) for all sufficiently large k. Hence

\[
Z_p(\psi_p,s)=\sum_k a_kp^{-ks}
\]

is a finite Laurent part plus one geometric tail, so

\[
D_p(s):=(1-p^{-s})Z_p(\psi_p,s)
\in\mathbb C[p^s,p^{-s}].
\]

This is an exact calculation, not an asymptotic.

Therefore any finite modification of finite places gives

\[
F_\varepsilon(s)=\xi(s)D_\varepsilon(s)
\]

with D entire Laurent–Dirichlet. If the deformation passes through the standard source, then \(D_0=1\), so for each fixed xi-zero \(\rho\), \(D_\varepsilon(\rho)\neq0\) for sufficiently small \(\varepsilon\). Thus the original zero and its exact multiplicity remain fixed locally.

**Conclusion.** Regular finite-place deformations are divisor-stationary, not merely first-order stationary.

#### C. Stronger global Tate theorem

The key strengthening is also correct.

A Schwartz–Bruhat function on A_Q is a finite linear combination of restricted pure tensors whose finite local component equals \(1_{\mathbb Z_p}\) at all but finitely many p. For the trivial local character, Tate local theory says every local zeta integral is the standard local L-factor times an entire local ratio. Thus for every pure tensor,

\[
Z(\phi,s)=\Lambda(s)A_\phi(s),
\qquad
\Lambda(s)=\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s)\zeta(s),
\]

with \(A_\phi\) entire. Finite sums preserve the common factor.

Now

\[
\Lambda(s)=\frac{2\xi(s)}{s(s-1)}.
\]

If \(Z(\phi,s)\) itself is entire, the simple endpoint poles of \(\Lambda\) at 0 and 1 must be cancelled by A, hence

\[
A_\phi(0)=A_\phi(1)=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
A_\phi(s)=s(s-1)B_\phi(s)
\]

with B entire, and

\[
\boxed{Z(\phi,s)=2\xi(s)B_\phi(s).}
\]

So in this category:

\[
\boxed{
\{\text{entire trivial-character Tate transforms}\}
\subset (\xi).
}
\]

This is a genuine category theorem.

#### D. Normal all-prime limits

Suppose finite-level transforms satisfy

\[
F_N=\xi D_N
\]

and converge locally uniformly near each xi-zero to an entire F. If \(\rho\) has multiplicity m, then every \(F_N^{(j)}(\rho)=0\) for \(0\le j<m\). Local uniform convergence plus Cauchy estimates gives convergence of derivatives, so the same vanishing holds for F. Hence F/xi has removable singularities at every xi-zero. Therefore normal holomorphic limits remain in the principal ideal.

This closure argument is correct.

The all-prime formal inversion example also identifies the obstruction correctly. The formal product of local unit indicators corresponds to \(1/\zeta\); in additive adelic measure, \(\prod_p\mathbb Z_p^\times\) has measure \(\prod_p(1-p^{-1})=0\), and its indicator is not a nonzero Schwartz–Bruhat vector. At first order, the prime-zeta function acquires logarithmic singularities at zeta zeros. Thus the escape requires loss of the regular category precisely where the zeros are located.

This does not prove every imaginable distributional enlargement is impossible. It proves regular/normal Tate escape is impossible.

#### E. Archimedean directions

For arbitrary Schwartz f on R, keep standard finite places and apply the same completion operator:

\[
\phi_f=\tfrac12E(E+1)f\otimes\prod_p1_{\mathbb Z_p}.
\]

Then

\[
Z(\phi_f,s)
=\tfrac12s(s-1)\zeta(s)Z_\infty(f,s)
=\xi(s)\frac{Z_\infty(f,s)}{\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s)}.
\]

Tate local theory gives that the quotient is entire for the trivial character. Therefore these regular archimedean source variations also remain in (xi).

If one instead perturbs the already-formed reciprocal kernel by a generic even entire Laplace transform, zeros can move and finite symmetry-compatible jet sets can be separated. But this enlargement no longer carries the arithmetic Tate source restriction. The report correctly classifies it as generic/non-rigid rather than as a Riemann arithmetic tangent.

#### F. Multiplicity-safe tangent

Let

\[
F_0(\rho+z)=z^mu(z),\quad u(0)\ne0,
\]

and

\[
F_\varepsilon=F_0+\varepsilon H+O(\varepsilon^2).
\]

Weierstrass preparation gives

\[
W_\varepsilon(z)=z^m+\varepsilon q(z)+O(\varepsilon^2),\quad \deg q<m.
\]

Differentiating the factorization yields

\[
q(z)\equiv H(z)/u(z)\pmod{z^m}.
\]

Hence the complete local divisor tangent is

\[
T_\rho(H)=[H/u]\in\mathbb C[z]/(z^m).
\]

The contour coefficient formula in the return is correct. No division by \(\xi'(\rho)\) is used.

For a regular Tate tangent \(H=DF_0\),

\[
H/u=Dz^m,
\]

so its class mod \(z^m\) is zero:

\[
\boxed{T_\rho(H)=0.}
\]

Thus the full multiplicity-safe tangent vanishes, not just the simple-root velocity.

#### G. Bombieri bridge

The report correctly distinguishes divisor motion from Bombieri’s coefficient null state. A family of local divisor tangents, even if finite-jet separating, does not by itself give a relation on an arbitrary coefficient vector in \(K_I\). In the regular Tate category the tangent is actually zero, so there is no bridge at all.

#### H. 反例审计

The generic reciprocal-kernel and positive/self-dual examples are valid firewalls against claiming that evenness, positivity, Fourier self-duality, or reciprocal symmetry alone create Riemann rigidity. They are not used to prove the principal-ideal theorem; they delimit the scope of possible escapes.

and entireness gives

\[
