## Round 195 — A/B result: the source-native arithmetic/gauge paper is conditional on an admitted source system

**Source inspected:** Salimah H. Meghani, *Source-Native Equivalence across
Arithmetic and Gauge Theory for the Riemann Hypothesis and the Yang–Mills Mass
Gap*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/21311229>, PDF downloaded as
/tmp/meghani-source-native.pdf.

The main theorem begins by fixing a compact regular source core
\(K_\alpha\), an equilibrium class, a completion lock, and a declared source
identity.  The claimed reconstruction
\[
 \Pi^{\rm cmp}_{A,\alpha}(x)=\Pi^{\rm cmp}_{A,\alpha}(y)
 \Longleftrightarrow x=y
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \Pi^{\rm cmp}_{G,\alpha}(x)=\Pi^{\rm cmp}_{G,\alpha}(y)
\]
is proved only on those admitted strata and after assuming the exact
source-native character-frame identities and the imported companion theorem
interfaces.  Those hypotheses are not derived from the standard definition
of \(\zeta\) or from an unconditional RH-bearing operator.

The arithmetic branch theorem extracts a finite character frame from a marked
primitive/determinant package.  It does not prove that this package equals the
completed zeta function or that its determinant zeros are the non-trivial
zeta zeros.  Indeed, Theorem 9.1 explicitly states that the scalar arithmetic
terminal map is constant on a fixed-lock stratum; that is an information-loss
statement about the declared model, not a positivity theorem for the Weil
form and not a localization theorem for zeta zeros.

Consequently the paper's mutual reconstruction theorem cannot supply the
missing arithmetic-to-\(\zeta\) identification.  Its conclusions are
conditional on the admitted source data and imported companion systems, and
no complete unconditional proof or disproof of RH follows.
