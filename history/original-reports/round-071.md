## Round 71 — the modular-KMS claim substitutes the Robin equivalence for a proof

I checked the public record for [*The Modular KMS Automorphism on Hyperfinite
Von Neumann Factors*](https://zenodo.org/records/20666636). The uploaded
publication is access-restricted; the public abstract is therefore the
source material that can be independently audited here.

The abstract explicitly invokes the Robin inequality as an “exact
mathematical equivalence” and then says that a first-order formal argument
establishes
\[
 \sigma(n)<e^\gamma n\log\log n\qquad(n>5040).
\tag{71.1}
\]
The equivalence between the Robin inequality and RH is itself an established
reformulation of RH. It does not supply an implication in either direction
unless (71.1) is proved by an independent arithmetic argument.

The public description gives no derivation of (71.1). It only names a
Bost--Connes Hamiltonian, a type-\(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor, a KMS symmetry, and
a second Chern class, then asserts that an off-axis zero would create a
“non-compensable isometric shear.” No algebra, operator domain, state, trace
identity, spectral determinant, or theorem connecting that shear to the
real parts of zeta zeros is supplied in the accessible material.

Thus the verifiable logical form is
\[
 \text{RH}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{Robin inequality}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\text{(unproved geometric assertion)}\Longrightarrow\text{Robin inequality}.
\tag{71.2}
\]
The second implication is the entire RH-bearing step and is not established.

**Established:** the record identifies a known equivalent criterion for RH.

**Failure / error:** naming that equivalence and an undefined KMS/Chern-class
obstruction does not prove the Robin inequality or exclude any off-critical
zero.

**Remaining load-bearing statement:** provide a complete, independently
checkable derivation of (71.1) from the stated noncommutative model, including
the precise representation, trace/KMS identity, and the step that converts
off-axis zero data into a contradiction. The public record does not provide
it.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
