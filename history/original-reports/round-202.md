## Round 202 — A/B result: the contradicted Proposition 6.1 breaks every dependent conclusion

The dependency statements in Velez v5 are explicit.  The proof of Theorem
7.1 says to choose \(X>e^R\) and then invokes Proposition 6.1 to conclude
\[
Q_{\rm arith}(F)=\|\Phi_X(F)\|_{H_X}^2\ge0.
\]
Corollary 7.2 derives a positive tempered measure from that conclusion, and
Theorem 8.1 invokes Theorem 7.1 together with Proposition 3.4 to claim RH.

Round 200 gives \(F,G\in A_0\) satisfying the support hypothesis for
Proposition 6.1 but
\[
q_X(F,G)=0,\qquad q_X(G,F)>0.
\]
Thus the proposition's asserted Hilbert-space identity is false on its
declared domain.  Round 196 independently shows that Proposition 3.4's
prime-side identity also contradicts the definitions.  Therefore the
proofs of Theorem 7.1, Corollary 7.2, and Theorem 8.1 do not establish their
stated conclusions.

No later positivity, measure, Weil-criterion, or RH conclusion depending on
these statements is counted as proved.
