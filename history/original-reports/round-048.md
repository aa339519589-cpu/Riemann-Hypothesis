## Round 48 — scope correction for the \(d_\gamma\geq\tfrac14\) estimate

The estimate \(d_\gamma=\tfrac14+\gamma^2\geq\tfrac14\) is a real-positive
estimate only on the critical coordinates, where \(\gamma\in\mathbb R\). It
must not be stated for a general off-critical complex ordinate. The precise
form of the argument is:

\[
 d_\gamma\geq\tfrac14\quad(\gamma\in\Gamma_C),
\]

while the off-critical set \(E\) is finite and \(d_\gamma\neq0\) for every
nontrivial zeta zero. Consequently the critical sequence
\[
 q_\gamma=\frac{w_\gamma}{\sqrt{d_\gamma}}
 \quad(\gamma\in\Gamma_C)
\]
is in \(\ell^2(\Gamma_C)\), and adjoining the finitely many exceptional
coordinates preserves \(\ell^2\) after choosing any square-root branch for
each nonzero complex \(d_\gamma\). All norm estimates in Round 47 use only the
critical inequality and are therefore valid as written.

This corrects the unrestricted wording in Round 44; it does not change the
finite-exception range relation (47.11) or the unresolved range-exclusion
problem.
