## Round 340 — Velez’s stated pole subspace is not a subspace of the stated \(L^2\) space

Proposition 6.1 defines \(\mathcal H_X=L^2(C_X,d^\times u)\ominus
\mathcal H_X^{0,1}\) and calls \(\mathcal H_X^{0,1}\) the two-dimensional
subspace of the characters \(|u|^0\) and \(|u|^1\). As written, these characters
are not vectors in that \(L^2\) space. The module map has a noncompact
\(\mathbb R_{>0}\) direction. Choose a compact positive-measure set in its kernel
and a measurable section of the module coordinate. For \(\alpha=0,1\), the norm
of \(|u|^\alpha\) then has the lower bound
\[
\|\,|u|^\alpha\,\|_2^2
\ \ge c\int_1^\infty r^{2\alpha}\,\frac{dr}{r}=\infty.
\]
Thus neither \(|u|^0\) nor \(|u|^1\) belongs to \(L^2(C_X,d^\times u)\); they
could only be handled as generalized vectors after specifying a rigged-space or
cutoff quotient construction. The manuscript supplies no such construction and no
proof that the resulting pole removal is a closed orthogonal projection. Therefore
the Hilbert-space expression used to state Proposition 6.1 is not defined by the
definitions given, independently of any question about the truth of RH.
