## Round 192 — A/B result: Migdal's turbulence manuscript explicitly conditions its zeta analysis on RH

**Source inspected:** Alexander Migdal, *Decaying Turbulence and the Riemann
Hypothesis: The number theory behind the infinite-time singularity*, arXiv
v6, DOI
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12207>, PDF downloaded as
/tmp/migdal-rh-v6.pdf.

The manuscript's abstract and its Section 7 explicitly state the hypothesis
that the Riemann Hypothesis and simplicity of the non-trivial zeros hold before
deriving the Riemann-wall poles, Stokes activations, and activation-time
asymptotics.  Theorem 7.1 has the same conditional premise.  Its pole notation
uses zeros of the form (1/2+i\rho_n) as input data; it does not derive that
form for all zeros.

The final discussion also treats the off-critical case as a hypothetical
alternative producing anomalous poles and explicitly characterizes the result
as a reformulation of consequences of RH, rather than a proof.  Neither the
formal turbulence construction nor the finite DNS comparison supplies a
mathematical implication excluding those hypothetical poles.  Thus the
manuscript provides no unconditional theorem in the direction required for
RH.
