## Round 76 — the transition-layer sequel identifies, but does not solve, the global positivity problem

I also checked the sequel [*Curvature Transition Layers and Paired-Tail
Positivity for the Riemann \(\Xi\)-Function*](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202606.1957).
Its status labels are important: the paper itself marks several central
steps as conditional or numerical.

The proposed Riccati route assumes
\[
 p'(u)>0,\qquad
 R(u)=2p(u)p'(u)-p''(u)>0
\quad(u>0),
\tag{76.1}
\]
and uses these assumptions to control the transition geometry. The paper
states that analytic proofs of the global inequalities in (76.1) remain
open. Its compact interval checks and tail estimates do not replace the
missing proof on the full half-line.

More importantly, the manuscript explicitly leaves the pre-crest block
positivity
\[
 C_m^{\mathrm{tot}}(x,y)>0
\tag{76.2}
\]
as an open conjectural step, and calls the bridge from complete block
positivity to \(I(x,y)>0\), hence to RH, conditional. Positivity of the
post-crest blocks for large \(x\), or numerical positivity on a bounded
rectangle, cannot imply (76.2) for every \(x>0\) and \(y>0\). The
oscillatory sine factors change sign, so local positivity near the diagonal
does not dominate the unbounded family of pre-crest blocks.

**Established:** the change of variables, paired-block identities, and
some asymptotic remainder estimates are useful reductions of the theta-kernel
problem.

**Remaining load-bearing statement:** prove the global Riccati inequalities,
all pre-crest block signs, the uniform summation of block remainders, and the
unconditional growth-equivalence bridge. The sequel does not close any of
these RH-bearing steps.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
