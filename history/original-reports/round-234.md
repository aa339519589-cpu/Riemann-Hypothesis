## Round 234 — A/B result: Aksman's thermodynamic Weil argument assumes the full positivity bridge

**Source inspected:** M. I. Aksman, *Thermodynamic Positivity and the
Riemann Hypothesis: A Vorton-Vacuum Realization of Weil's Explicit Formula
on an Open Arithmetic Boundary*, Zenodo version 2,
<https://zenodo.org/records/21937318>, PDF pp. 1–6.

The standard implication “full Weil positivity implies RH” is not the
missing result here. The manuscript's decisive bridge is the claimed map
\(A:\mathcal T_{\mathrm{Weil}}\to\mathcal H_{\mathrm{vac}}\), together with
the exact identity relating the vacuum second variation to \(W(f)\), and the
asserted Cycle-Cone Closure
\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{physical}}=\mathcal T_{\mathrm{Weil}}.
\]
Section 9 does not prove this equality. It gives no definition of the
physical cone with a topology, no construction showing surjectivity onto the
Schwartz test space, and no continuity/closedness theorem that could turn
density into equality. The statement that a finite DSM-861 core supplies
“infinite-dimensional capacity” is an assertion, not a mathematical
derivation.

The final theorem makes the gap explicit: it begins with
\[
\delta^2F[u_0](A(f),A(f))=C\,W(f)\quad(C>0)
\]
for every \(f\in\mathcal T_{\mathrm{Weil}}\) as an assumption. That is
essentially the load-bearing positivity-transfer theorem required to reach
RH; it is not established by the preceding Ginzburg--Landau expansion or
by the Arakelov sign statement on a restricted collection of cycles.
Likewise, “the vacuum is definitively stable” is a physical premise, not a
proved property of a specified self-adjoint Hessian on the full test space.

There is also no closed analytic treatment of the open cusp. The text first
retains the continuous Eisenstein scattering contribution, then says that a
Birman--Krein deformation “neutralizes” it and makes the spectrum strictly
positive and discrete, without defining the deformation domain or proving
the claimed spectral and torsion bounds. This unsupported step cannot
establish the sign of \(B_\infty(f)\) for all Weil tests.

Thus the source restates the Weil criterion and proposes a physical
interpretation of its positivity, but its final conclusion is conditional
on the unproved realization, closure, stability, and exact-identity
claims. It supplies no unconditional RH proof or disproof.

\[
\boxed{\text{UNPROVED LOAD-BEARING CLAIM}}
\]

本步没有产生新的无条件 RH 证明进展。
