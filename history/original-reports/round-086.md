## Round 86 — a model shows that real frequencies and the Bombieri weight do not force range exclusion

There is a concrete countermodel to any proposed exclusion based only on
realness of the frequencies, completeness, and the algebraic weight. Take
\(\Lambda=\mathbb Z\) and a proper interval \(I=(-a,a)\) with \(0<a<\pi\).
Choose a smooth \(2\pi\)-periodic function \(G\) that agrees with
\[
g(u)=e^{bu},\qquad b\ne0,
\tag{86.1}
\]
on \(I\); for example, multiply \(e^{bu}\) by a smooth cutoff that equals one
on \([-a,a]\) and vanishes near the endpoints of \([-\pi,\pi]\), then extend
periodically. Its Fourier coefficients \(c_n\) decrease faster than every
power of \(|n|\). Define
\[
w_n:=\left(\frac14+n^2\right)c_n.
\tag{86.2}
\]
Then \(w\in\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\), and the weighted real-frequency synthesis
\[
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}
\frac{w_n}{\frac14+n^2}e^{-inu}
=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}c_ne^{-inu}
=G(u)
\tag{86.3}
\]
converges smoothly. Restricting to \(I\) gives
\[
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}
\frac{w_n}{\frac14+n^2}e^{-inu}
=e^{bu}
=e^{-i(ib)u}.
\tag{86.4}
\]
The right side is a non-real-frequency exponential, yet it lies exactly in
the weighted range generated by real frequencies. The construction can be
rescaled to any prescribed interval length.

This model is not a statement about the actual zeta zero set. Its role is
structural: no theorem depending only on the facts that the synthesis
frequencies are real, the system is complete on \(I\), and the coefficients
carry the Bombieri \(1/(\frac14+\gamma^2)\) weight can establish (85.6).
Actual exclusion would have to use additional arithmetic information about
the particular critical ordinates and the particular off-line packet,
stronger than the BM density data and stronger than compactness.

**Updated route boundary:** the exact weighted range target remains the right
formulation of the surviving alternative, but it is not a generic harmonic
analysis consequence. Any successful proof must identify and exploit a
zeta-specific invariant that is preserved by the critical synthesis and
violated by every off-line exponential packet.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
