## Round 156 — the missing scalar bridge has the full strength of the determinant conclusion

The omitted statement is not a harmless choice of coordinates.  If the
ordinary scalar coefficient identity requested in Lemma 6.129 were proved and
the stated finite-window limits were valid, then the existing comparison
chain would give

\[
 \lim_{M\to\infty}
 \left\langle\mu^{\mathrm{fp,sc}}_{R,M},
 \Psi^{\mathrm{sc,fw}}_{w,M}\right\rangle
 =G_K(w)
 =\partial_w\log\det_2(I+iwK).
\tag{156.1}
\]

The finite-window comparison with the classical explicit-formula ledger would
simultaneously give

\[
 \lim_{M\to\infty}
 \left\langle\mu^{\mathrm{sc}}_{\mathrm{EF},M},
 \Psi^{\mathrm{sc,fw}}_{w,M}\right\rangle
 =
 \partial_w\log\xi\!\left(\frac12+w\right)
 -\partial_w\log\xi\!\left(\frac12\right).
\tag{156.2}
\]

Equating (156.1) and (156.2), then integrating in \(w\), gives
\[
 \frac{F_K(\frac12+w)}{\xi(\frac12+w)}
 =\frac{F_K(\frac12)}{\xi(\frac12)}.
\]
The central scalar normalization makes the right side \(1\), so local
analytic equality and the identity theorem give \(F_K\equiv\xi\).  Since the
operator \(K\) is self-adjoint, its determinant zeros are on
\(\operatorname{Re}s=\frac12\).  Thus the missing ordinary-scalar bridge is
already an RH-sufficient theorem.

This also rules out a circular repair.  If one defines a
\(K_M\)-dependent projection \(P^{\mathrm{sc}}_{M,N}\) so that
\[
 \left\langle\mu^{\mathrm{fp,sc}}_{R,M},
 P^{\mathrm{sc}}_{M,N}\Psi^{\mathrm{sc}}_{M,\ell}\right\rangle
 =\operatorname{tr}(K_{M,N}^{\ell}),
\]
then one has defined the determinant-side moments into the scalar channel.
To use that definition in the RH proof, one must still prove
\[
 P^{\mathrm{sc}}_{M,N}\Psi^{\mathrm{sc}}_{M,\ell}
 \longrightarrow \Psi^{\mathrm{sc}}_{M,\ell}
\]
in a topology for which the ordinary scalar functional is continuous, and
prove that the limit is the coefficient generated by
\(h_w(u)=(e^{wu}-1)/u\).  Those are exactly the missing independent
statements; without them the construction is only a re-labelled determinant
trace.
