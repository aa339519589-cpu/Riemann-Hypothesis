## Round 228 — A/B result: PIHR Part IX closes fixed-parameter asymptotics but leaves the critical boundary layer unresolved

**Source inspected:** Borros Arneth, *Asymptotic Selberg–Memory Dominance,
Alzer–Kwong Equilibrium Control, and Boundary-Layer Reduction of the Critical
Beta Closure*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22111643>, PDF pp. 1–2, 14–20, 24–27.

The manuscript proves, at its stated level, positive asymptotic comparison for
each fixed Selberg order and for each fixed deformation parameter \(\beta>0\).
Those statements do not give the uniform inequality needed at the critical
endpoint. In the critical double scaling \(\beta=\lambda/s\), the paper proves
only the common leading limit

\[
P_{\lambda/s}(s),\,R_{\lambda/s}(s)\longrightarrow
\gamma(e^\lambda-1).
\]

Consequently the sign of \(P_\beta(s)-R_\beta(s)\) is decided one order
deeper. The proposed first-order boundary-layer limit

\[
s\bigl(P_{\lambda/s}(s)-R_{\lambda/s}(s)\bigr)
\longrightarrow
\lambda\bigl[\gamma(\log16-\gamma)+
\kappa(e^\lambda-1)\bigr]
\]

is explicitly labelled Candidate Theorem 8 and is stated to be unproved. The
paper identifies the missing uniform remainder control needed to resum the
fixed-order asymptotics in the diagonal region \(1\ll m\ll s\log s\).

The manuscript's own “What Remains Open” section says that the fixed-\(\beta\)
threshold is not uniform as \(\beta\downarrow0\), that the double-scaling
theorem determines only the equal leading term, and that the next-order
positivity is required before the comparison can be propagated to the RH
criterion. Its conclusion again says that RH remains unproved. Thus the
fixed-parameter results are genuine auxiliary asymptotics, but they do not
close the endpoint quantifier and yield no unconditional RH proof or
disproof.
