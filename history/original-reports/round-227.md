## Round 227 — A/B result: PIHR Part XII derives an exact prime-flow recursion but leaves the Bellman barrier unproved

**Source inspected:** Borros Arneth, *Renormalized Prime-Flow Dynamics,
Periodic Memory Tails, and Square-Root Bellman Descent for the Critical
Half-Mode*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22130771>, PDF pp. 1–10 and 20–25.

The manuscript reduces a critical half-mode inequality to an exact weighted
area identity and derives exact finite-prime adjunction and renormalized
recursion formulas. It does not prove the sign of the terminal half-mode
margin. The proposed Bellman invariant is introduced only as a desired
function/barrier: the manuscript says that an invariant lower barrier would
have to be constructed and that the Bellman functional is presently a
programme, not a theorem.

The square-root descent \(x\mapsto\sqrt{x}\mapsto x^{1/4}\mapsto\cdots\)
does not by itself yield positivity. The descent formula expresses a delayed
state in terms of the complete margin at a smaller argument, but no proved
inequality controls the accumulated finite-prime production, memory term, and
the terminal limit with the required sign. The paper explicitly identifies
the missing invariant prime-flow Bellman functional as its principal open
problem.

The final discussion also distinguishes the exact recurrence and periodic
finite-state identities from the desired global conclusion. Its abstract and
conclusion expressly state that RH is not proved. A finite Euler sieve,
periodic averaging, or a numerical falsifier can test a proposed barrier but
cannot establish the universal inequality for all \(x\).

Thus this manuscript supplies exact auxiliary identities and a reformulation
of an RH-strength positivity problem, but no new unconditional load-bearing
theorem for RH and no disproof.
