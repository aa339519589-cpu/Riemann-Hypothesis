## Round 99 — the new Liouville--Riesz criterion is one-way

The August 27, 2026 paper [*On variants of Pólya's conjecture*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27130) studies
\[
f(x)=-\sum_{n\le x}\frac{\lambda(n)\log n}{\sqrt n}\log\frac{x}{n}.
\tag{99.1}
\]
Its unconditional implication is only
\[
f(x)\ge0\ \text{eventually}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mathrm{RH}.
\tag{99.2}
\]
The paper's converse and its asymptotic formula for \(f(x)\) explicitly assume RH, the Simple Zero Conjecture, and an additional absolute-convergence condition on the zero expansion. Hence no unconditional proof of the premise in (99.2) is supplied. Establishing eventual nonnegativity would itself be a new RH-bearing theorem, not a consequence of the paper's Mellin transform identity.

**Established:** this is a valid one-way sign criterion, not an unconditional closure of RH.

**Route decision:** retain the Bombieri weighted-null-cone exclusion as the active target.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
