## Round 229 — A/B result: Devine's 67.3399% theorem is a proportion bound, not a zero-location theorem

**Source inspected:** Michael Devine, *An Unconditional 67.3399% Bound and
Conditional Advances Beyond 67.92% for Simple Critical Zeros of the Riemann
Zeta Function*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22066689>, PDF pp. 1–3, 7–9, 15–16.

The unconditional main theorem gives only

\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_{0,s}(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}\ge0.673399.
\]

This asserts that a positive proportion of zeros are simple and on the
critical line. It does not constrain the real parts of the remaining zeros.
The paper's own scope section says that the theorem does not constrain
horizontal zero location. The finite profile certificates and the fixed-kernel
ceiling are statements about the sampling method, not about the true zero
set.

The manuscript's stronger results are explicitly conditional: BOX-DENS,
PROFILE-DENS, or a related averaged depth condition is assumed before the
transfer estimate and the \(0.6792+\varepsilon_f\) conclusion. Those
hypotheses are not known unconditionally and, even if granted, still give a
density statement rather than the assertion that every nontrivial zero has
real part \(1/2\). The source expressly states that none of its results proves
RH.

Thus the paper supplies an unconditional partial proportion theorem and
conditional refinements, but no unconditional load-bearing theorem for RH
and no disproof.

\[
\boxed{\text{\rm UNPROVED LOAD-BEARING CLAIM}}
\]

本步没有产生新的无条件 RH 证明进展。
