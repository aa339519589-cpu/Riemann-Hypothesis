## Round 174 — the new geometric and finite-field claims remain programmatic or conditional

Two additional 2026 records returned by the current search do not close the
RH implication.

First, Aksman's *The Riemann Hypothesis Program* is explicitly a poster and
describes itself as a “proof program”:

<https://zenodo.org/records/22063440>

Its central paragraph says that uniform porosity, an FUP, and the exact
automorphic scattering transfer are the three bridges, and that the
conclusion follows “once written as explicit lemmas and composed”.  The record
does not contain those completed lemmas or a proof that its proposed trapped
set is the arithmetic scattering system for \(\zeta\).  The displayed chain

\[
 \text{Vorton geometry}\Rightarrow\text{porosity}\Rightarrow\text{FUP}
 \Rightarrow\text{resonance gap}\Rightarrow\Re\rho=\tfrac12
\tag{174.1}
\]

is therefore an agenda, not a derived implication.  In particular, a generic
resonance gap cannot be transferred to zeta zeros without an independently
proved equality of scattering determinants.

Second, the finite-field manuscript *A Finite Field Realisation of the
Riemann Hypothesis* states its own limitation:

<https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202606.0768/v1>

It constructs a self-adjoint finite-shell operator, but explicitly makes the
RH conclusion conditional on a reductio that declares the finite shell to be
the entire ontology and excludes an external continuum.  Without that
premise, the paper says that the construction reduces RH to the “faithfulness
of the de-framing”.  A self-adjoint operator therefore proves reality of the
constructed finite spectrum only; it does not prove that this spectrum equals
all zeros of the classical \(\zeta\)-function.  That faithfulness/equality is
the missing arithmetic bridge, not a consequence of self-adjointness.

**Established:** neither record supplies an unconditional map from its
constructed geometric or finite spectrum to every non-trivial zero of
\(\zeta\); one is explicitly a program and the other is explicitly
conditional.

**Remaining load-bearing statement:** prove the porosity--FUP--scattering
lemmas with an exact zeta determinant identity, or prove unconditional
faithfulness of the finite-field de-framing.  Neither is present.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
