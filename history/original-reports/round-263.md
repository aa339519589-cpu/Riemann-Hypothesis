## Round 263 — B-result: the quantum-enhanced candidate is a proposal, not an RH theorem

Source inspected: Jincheng Zhang, *Quantum-Enhanced Riemann Hypothesis via
Entangled Variables*, Zenodo record
[22152880](https://zenodo.org/records/22152880), PDF SHA-256
45a7639e2bd2c0eb8a23fc2e45ad01cf245e3efafa744b8f0ac8f4ff8e63a090.
The record's own description calls the manuscript a proposed
quantum/classical algorithm and lists possible future outcomes; it does not
claim a completed proof.

The paper never defines a quantum observable or a mathematically specified
map
\[
\text{quantum state and measurement data}\longmapsto\zeta(s)
\]
whose expectation, variance, or limiting distribution would imply that every
nontrivial zero has real part \(1/2\). The “Entangled Probe” is only an
outline: initialization, unspecified quantum operations, measurements,
Fourier/statistical analysis, optimization, and iteration. No circuit,
operator, estimator, error bound, convergence theorem, or implication from a
measured pattern to a zero-location theorem is supplied. A finite or
eventually unbounded computational search would not establish a universal
statement about all zeros in any case.

The mathematical setup also contains incorrect definitions of the target
problem, including the claim that nontrivial zeros have the form
\((\pm1+1/2)^p\) and a later assertion resembling
\(\zeta(s)=1/16+x\). These are not the Riemann zeta function or the Riemann
Hypothesis. Consequently there is no load-bearing analytic theorem to
verify, no unconditional disproof, and no proof chain contribution.

\[
\boxed{\text{UNPROVED LOAD-BEARING CLAIM}}
\]

本步没有产生新的无条件 RH 证明进展。
