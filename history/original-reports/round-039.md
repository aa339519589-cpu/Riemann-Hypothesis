## Round 39 — source-faithful correction to the form-core statement

The exact core statement used in the finite-section audit is the following:

\[
 QW_\lambda(f,f)
 =\liminf_{g_j\to f,\;g_j\in E}QW_\lambda(g_j,g_j),
\tag{39.1}
\]

where \(E\) is the full span of the logarithmic Fourier modes. It is not an
automatic equality with \(QW_\lambda^N(f_N,f_N)\) for an arbitrary named
sequence of finite truncations. Such an equality for a chosen \(f_N\) needs a
separate form-domain argument controlling the weighted archimedean Fourier
term and the changing arithmetic cutoff. This correction strengthens, rather
than changes, the Round-33 audit: Fejér convergence in the underlying
\(L^2\)-norm is insufficient to supply the needed form convergence.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
