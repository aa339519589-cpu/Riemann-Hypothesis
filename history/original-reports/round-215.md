## Round 215 — A/B result: Aksman's Fredholm-scattering causality manuscript asserts, but does not prove, the pole-exclusion theorem

**Source inspected:** Mikhail I. Aksman, *A Fredholm--Scattering
Formulation of the Hilbert--Pólya Program*, August 2026, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22149909>, PDF pp. 1--4.

The manuscript does not define the claimed Mosco-limit operator, its domain,
the orbifold group, or the compactification on which the subsequent diameter
and causality statements are supposed to hold.  It explicitly acknowledges
that self-adjointness controls the point spectrum but not scattering
resonances; the missing conclusion is therefore its separate “Universal
Automorphic Causality Exclusion” theorem.

Equations (3)--(7) compute the phase derivative of one formal Blaschke factor
under the assumption that an off-line pole is present.  They do not prove the
load-bearing inequality used next,
\(\tau_{\rm reg}\ge -2D_{\max}\).  Subtracting a cusp phase changes the time
delay, so the ordinary finite-range Wigner bound cannot simply be transferred
to \(\tau_{\rm reg}\) without a theorem.  The manuscript gives no definition
or calculation proving a compactified bulk with \(D_{\max}<4\), and it gives
no estimate controlling the background and the other scattering factors in
the total regularized delay.  A local pole contribution alone is not a bound
on that total quantity.

The claimed contradiction therefore depends on two unproved premises: the
regularized causality bound and the geometric diameter bound.  Neither follows
from the displayed Blaschke algebra.  In addition, this four-page manuscript
provides no exact identity of its scattering determinant with \(\xi(2s)\) (or
any equivalent completed-zeta determinant), nor a proved bijection between
all zeta zeros and its resonances.  The abstract's assertion that the
determinant “factors” the completed zeta function is not a derivation.

Thus Theorem 4.1 is an asserted zero-exclusion statement, not an established
unconditional theorem, and the final RH implication has no valid spectral
map.
