## Round 225 — A/B result: Mittermeier's terminal-episode theorem removes only a reduction branch and leaves the RH-strength universal inequality open

**Source inspected:** Rainer Andreas Mittermeier, *Deep Episodes in the
Prime-Power Checkpoint Program: An Unconditional Terminal-Episode Theorem, an
Exact Chebyshev Bridge, and Sharp Recovery Recurrence — Part 5*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22076088>, PDF pp. 2–10.

The paper explicitly separates its unconditional result from the remaining
Riemann-Hypothesis statement. Theorem 3.5 proves only that a terminal active
episode cannot persist forever. Its introduction lists the two tail clauses:
the terminal episode clause, which the paper addresses, and the reserve
inequality

\[
V_q\ge0
\]

at the recovery witness of every episode that later recovers. The latter is
not proved. Corollary 3.9 states that RH is equivalent to this remaining
inequality at every recovery witness; this is a quantifier reduction, not a
proof of the inequality.

There is also an explicit logical warning inside the proof of Theorem 3.5.
Under the assumption of a terminal episode and the resulting eventual
positivity/integrability of the shifted weighted Chebyshev error, Case 2
temporarily derives that all nontrivial zeros lie on the critical line, and
then obtains a contradiction from the pole at a nontrivial zero. Remark 3.6
correctly says that this intermediate critical-line conclusion is conditional
on the terminal-episode assumption and is not an assertion about the actual
zeros. It therefore cannot serve as an RH proof.

The exact Chebyshev bridge, finite verification through \(q=10^{10}\),
recovery recurrence, and conditional drawdown estimates do not close the
universal reserve inequality. The abstract and Section 1 expressly identify
that inequality as the remaining open problem and state that RH remains open.

Thus the source proves a genuine auxiliary statement about the checkpoint
dynamics, but no new unconditional load-bearing theorem for RH and no
disproof.

\[
\boxed{\text{\rm UNPROVED LOAD-BEARING CLAIM}}
\]

本步没有产生新的无条件 RH 证明进展。
