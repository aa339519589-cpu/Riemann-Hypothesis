## Round 218 — A/B result: PIHR--RH XV proves decay below the RH-sensitive scale and explicitly leaves the sharp barrier open

**Source inspected:** Borros Arneth, *Crossing Chebyshev--Riesz Reduction,
Selberg--Bregman Energy, Euler-Sieve Memory Decay, and the Sharp Critical
Barrier for the Riemann Hypothesis* (PIHR--RH XV), August 2026, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22156735>, PDF pp. 1--29.

This manuscript is explicit about the status of its strongest unconditional
results.  It proves a boundary decay statement of the form
\(M(x)/\sqrt{x}\to0\) and an \(o(1)\) estimate for the dangerous positive
large-prime memory.  It then checks that a hypothetical off-critical zero can
produce a normalized contribution that also tends to zero while remaining
larger than the \(x^{-1/2}\)-scale barrier required to detect it.  Therefore
the decay theorem does not imply the sign condition needed for RH.

The exact remaining condition is stated as the signed sharp-barrier
inequality for the completed Euler state.  The paper's Conjecture 1 is an
unproved Euler--Bellman theorem intended to preserve that barrier.  Its
mathematical-status section separately lists as unproved the crossing
Chebyshev--Riesz inequality on all late crossing cells, eventual positivity of
the transport state, eventual positivity of \(M(x)\), the signed barrier
theorem, and the final arithmetic step to RH.  The computational section
labels its finite tests as falsification tests only.

Thus the unconditional decay and exact reductions in PIHR--RH XV do not close
the RH-sensitive sign inequality or provide a complete RH proof.
