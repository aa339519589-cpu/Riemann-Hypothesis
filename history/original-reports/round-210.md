## Round 210 — A/B result: Derscariu--von Mangoldt v9 explicitly leaves finite off-line exceptions unresolved

**Source inspected:** Radu-Daniel Derscariu, *Proof of the Riemann
Hypothesis via the Derscariu--von Mangoldt Operator*, Version 9, DOI
<https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20430397>; the indexed record is reproduced at
<https://www.scivora.io/articles/proof-of-the-riemann-hypothesis-via-the-derscariuvon-mangoldt-operator-v9-2026>.

The source's own abstract states that its RH result is conditional on
excluding a finite number of off-critical zeros.  It then states that the
remaining obstacle is precisely to prove that an entire function with the
listed symmetry, order, and GUE pair correlation cannot have finitely many
off-critical zeros.  That is the missing implication required to pass from
all-but-finitely-many to none.

Consequently, even accepting every operator, trace, and asymptotic assertion
in the abstract, the source does not establish the zero-exclusion statement
\[
\forall\rho\;(\zeta(\rho)=0,\ 0<\Re\rho<1)
\Longrightarrow \Re\rho=\frac12.
\]
Its finite-exception condition is not an unconditional proof of RH, and the
reported finite numerical experiment cannot close that condition.
