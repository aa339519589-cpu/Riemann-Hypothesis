## Round 65 — even density one would not be the pointwise RH conclusion

The distinction between a proportion theorem and RH is exact. A statement of
the form

\[
 \frac{N_0(T)}{N(T)}\longrightarrow1
\tag{65.1}
\]

still permits finitely many off-critical zeros, or infinitely many such zeros
whose counting function is \(o(N(T))\). The same is true for a statement that
all but \(o(N(T))\) zeros are simple and on the critical line. No asymptotic
rank--trace certificate can therefore replace the pointwise implication

\[
 \zeta(\beta+i\gamma)=0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \beta=\tfrac12
 \tag{65.2}
\]

for every nontrivial zero, including arbitrary multiplicity.

In the Bombieri formulation, (65.2) requires either global positivity of the
Weil form on every admissible compact support or a direct exclusion of the
finite/infinite exceptional weighted-range branch. The rank--trace result in
Round 64 addresses neither: it is an asymptotic aggregate estimate, whereas
(61.3) is an exact local value-range relation.

**Established:** density \(1\), even if obtained, is strictly weaker than the
required pointwise RH statement.

**Failure / error:** converting an \(o(N(T))\) exceptional set into an empty
set is invalid.

**Unknown / next verification:** a pointwise arithmetic coercivity theorem
for the Weil/Bombieri form, or an exact exclusion of the exceptional
weighted-range relation.

**RH status:** no unconditional proof has been produced.
