## Round 226 — A/B result: PIHR Part V states its decisive macro-cycle inequality as a conjecture

**Source inspected:** Borros Arneth, *PIHR Critical-Weight Macro-Cycle
Candidate Proof of RH*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22064534>, PDF pp. 1–3, 6–14, 20–30.

The manuscript constructs several exact identities for a prime-driven scalar
state, including the interaction cancellation and the event recurrence. These
identities do not establish the required sign. The paper itself introduces
“PIHR Exponential Compensation” as Conjecture 1, namely eventual
nonnegativity of the propagated state \(H(t)\), and then introduces the
weighted macro-cycle inequality as Conjecture 2 / the Candidate Theorem.

In the notation of the manuscript, the missing assertion is the universal
inequality

\[
e^{-(\tau_b-\tau_a)/2}S_a+\mathcal R_{a,b}-\mathcal L_{a,b}\ge0
\]

for every sufficiently late complete deficit excursion, together with the
finite-transition and concatenation controls. The manuscript explicitly says
that proving this inequality is the decisive unproved arithmetic step. Its
own circularity audit lists \(H(t)\ge0\) eventually, \(M(t)\le2+\gamma\), and
the macro-cycle inequality as statements that remain unproved and may not be
assumed.

The later Laplace-transform and Landau argument is conditional on that missing
inequality: it would exclude zeros to the right of \(1/2\), after which the
functional equation would give RH. The conclusion repeats that the programme
reduces to proving the macro-cycle theorem and expressly claims no proof of it
or of RH. Numerical falsifiability tests and the exact recurrence cannot
replace the universal asymptotic inequality.

Therefore the manuscript supplies a reduction and exact bookkeeping, but its
only RH-load-bearing statement is still a conjecture; no unconditional RH
proof or disproof is obtained.

\]
