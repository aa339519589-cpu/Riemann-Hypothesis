## Round 244 — A/B result: the PIHR macro-cycle manuscript leaves its critical inequality unproved

**Source inspected:** Borros Arneth, *PIHR Critical-Weight Macro-Cycle
Candidate Proof of RH*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/22064534>, PDF pp. 1--31.
Downloaded source SHA-256:
55f0230a891ebb288dfc6a3a84338c143f989bda9b738930da53564cfcd05f1a.

The manuscript supplies several exact reductions from its unconditional
input \(D(t)=t-\sum_{n\le e^t}\Lambda(n)/n\ge0\): the positive auxiliary
state \(L\), the interaction cancellation \(M=D+L/2\), strict convexity of
\(M\) between prime-power events, and the exact safety-margin recurrence
\[
S_{k+1}=e^{-r_k/2}S_k+(1-e^{-r_k/2})\delta_k-\kappa(r_k),
\qquad
\kappa(r)=r-2(1-e^{-r/2})>0.
\]
These identities do not imply \(S_k\ge0\). The manuscript explicitly
isolates the missing assertion as the PIHR Critical-Weight Macro-Cycle
Theorem: for every sufficiently late complete deficit excursion \(C=[a,b]\),
\[
e^{-(\tau_b-\tau_a)/2}S_a+\mathcal R_C-\mathcal L_C\ge0,
\]
with enough control of transition regions to concatenate the cycles. It
states that the proof of this inequality is the “decisive unproved
arithmetic step” and later concludes that no proof of the theorem, and
therefore no proof of RH, is claimed.

The manuscript’s own circularity audit also lists \(H(t)\ge0\) eventually,
the equivalent ceiling \(M(t)\le2+\gamma\), the macro-cycle inequality,
and the Gamma-2 ceiling as unproved. The subsequent Landau argument is
conditional on that missing ceiling. Thus the exact recurrence and the
noncancellation calculation are not a new load-bearing RH theorem.
