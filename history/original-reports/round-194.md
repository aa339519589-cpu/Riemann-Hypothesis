## Round 194 — A/B result: Jaiswal's claimed disproof supplies no off-critical zero

**Source inspected:** Sandeep S. Jaiswal, *Riemann Hypothesis: The Most
Definitive and Comprehensive Disproof Ever Constructed*, Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/19733292>, PDF downloaded as
/tmp/jaiswal-rh-disproof.pdf.

The manuscript's first alleged contradiction confuses the defining Dirichlet
series with its analytic continuation.  The series
\[
 \sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}
\]
indeed converges only for \(\operatorname{Re}s>1\), but the analytically
continued zeta function is a different continuation of that function and is
defined in the critical strip.  Therefore nonconvergence of the original
series at \(\operatorname{Re}s=1/2\) does not contradict zeros of the
continued function.

The purported disproof then simply asserts the existence of points
\(s_n\) with \(\zeta(s_n)=0\) and \(\operatorname{Re}s_n\ne1/2\), without
constructing any such point or supplying a rigorous enclosure.  The
functional equation only maps a hypothetical off-line zero to its reflected
partner; it does not itself create one.  Likewise, deviations from GUE
statistics, violations of Gram's law, generalized-prime models, and finite
numerical observations do not produce a counterexample to the actual zeta
function.  No negative Li coefficient or other exact contradiction is
computed and proved.

Hence the manuscript establishes neither an off-critical zero nor a
mathematical contradiction to RH.  It is not an unconditional disproof.

\[
\boxed{\text{UNPROVED LOAD-BEARING CLAIM}}
\]

本步没有产生新的无条件 RH 证明进展。
